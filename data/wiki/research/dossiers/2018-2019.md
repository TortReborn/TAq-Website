## 1. The Federation: people (all [ATTESTED] unless noted)

- Guild: **Holders Of LE** [HoL]; forum title Nov 2018: **"Former Viceroy of the Foxes"** [TITLE, S1 p3 #42] — i.e. ex-Kingdom Foxes officer.
- Authored the Federation Tribute (S1, 2018-11-13), crediting ~25 people by name (below). Ascended Kitten on him: *"Drew gave them 9 months of constant xp and community"* (S3 p2 #24, 2018-11-11).
- Community historian: *"a massive ongoing written history of the guild community from May 2015 onwards"* kept on his Discord (S5, Drew1011 reply, 2019-06-27). **This Discord history is the single biggest untapped source for the era.**
