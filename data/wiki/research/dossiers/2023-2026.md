# Research dossier — 2023–2026, the private diplomacy era

> **CORRECTION (Aiden, 3 Sep 2026) — read the 2026 in-game alliance records narrowly.**
> Wynncraft's in-game ally feature is used mainly so guilds can run **guild raids
> together**; it is not a declaration of political allyship. Any inference in this
> dossier that treats an in-game ally entry as a bloc, a pact or a reconciliation
> is unsupported — in particular, the Avicia-listed-as-Sequoia-ally fixture is
> **not** evidence of a war-to-alliance arc. Cite these records only as
> game-mechanical relationships.

Compiled 2026-09-03. Rigor labels: **[ATTESTED]** = written, dated, sourced; **[RECOLLECTION]** = later testimony; **[INFERENCE]** = flagged as such; **[DEAD END]** = searched, nothing there.

**The one-line summary of this era:** public guild diplomacy stops being written down. The last Alliance-prefixed forum thread of all time is dated **21 Nov 2022**; between then and Sep 2026 there are **zero** alliance threads, one war-narrative video, and one guild newsletter that never names an enemy. Everything below is what survives.

- **[ATTESTED]** **TheNelston** (Idiot Co; ICo's former owner), thread 309201, **6 Jul 2023**, proposing an in-game alliance chat: *"This would allow for more community building between guilds that's currently very restricted to discord, and lets smaller guilds coordinate things much easier in game."* Three replies. - **[ATTESTED]** **Idiot Co's own recruitment thread** (301804), OP as edited **9 Aug 2025** by bumpjoinICo: *"As of 2/2/2024, ICo transitioned its application process to the discord."* The guilds closed their last public doors in early 2024.
- **[ATTESTED]** **Mocomochi**, "A comprehensive guide to guilds", thread 320194, **23 Dec 2024**: *"Submit an Application: Most guilds require an application, which can usually be found on their private Discord."* — by end-2024 "private Discord" is simply how the guide describes normal practice.

- **[ATTESTED]** **Ascended Kitten**, same thread, same day: *"I think the fact that there's fewer alliances and diplomacy going on is more owed to the change of war system."* — a competing causal explanation worth carrying into the era page.
- **[ATTESTED]** **Lucky32**, same thread, 29 Oct 2025: *"It's completely pointless and meaningless to start discussions concerning guilds or the guild community in 2025."*

## 5. In-game Diplomacy — the record reopens in 2026

The single most important structural finding: **Wynncraft has an in-game Diplomacy menu and alliance system**, and Sequoia's client mod parses it. That means alliance data becomes machine-readable again in 2026 after four dark years.

- **[ATTESTED]** Wynncraft wiki, Guild War page: *"this formula excludes territories owned by you, and will use the relevant tax rate based on your diplomacy with the territory owner. For instance, guilds in which you have an alliance with will tax you for the ally tax of that territory."* Alliances are now a **mechanical** relationship with an economic effect, not a social one.
- **[ATTESTED]** Sequoia-mod PR #61, merged **30 Jul 2026**, "feat: sync guild alliances and improve ingredient totems": *"report authoritative guild-alliance snapshots when Wynncraft diplomacy containers finish loading"*. Rationale, verbatim: *"Alliance chat events only report incremental changes and can drift from the backend's state. The diplomacy snapshot provides a complete reconciliation point whenever the player opens the populated guild diplomacy menu."*

### Real alliance events captured in the mod's test fixtures
From `ChatManagerTest.java` (archive `raw-githubusercontent-com-sequoiawynncraft-...`). These are the system-message strings the parser was built against — the closest thing this era has to a diplomatic record:

| Fixture string | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| `Sequoia formed an alliance with Silk Road` | Sequoia allied with **Silk Road [SILK]** |
| `Anime Lovers revoked the alliance with Sequoia` | **Anime Lovers** broke with Sequoia — *the only attested alliance breakdown of the entire era* |
| `GaztheCat revoked the alliance with Chiefs Of Corkus` | Sequoia's **owner personally** revoked the alliance with **Chiefs Of Corkus [HOC]** |
| `Tannslee formed an alliance with Radiant Roses` | A third-party alliance observed in passing: **Radiant Roses [RARO]** |
| `[NewM] captured the territory Detlas Suburbs.` | **New Moon** taking territory in a live war |

From `GuildAllianceSnapshotManagerTest.java`: the Diplomacy menu fixtures use **`Sequoia: Diplomacy`** and **`Nefarious Ravens: Diplomacy`** as menu titles, and the allied-guild entries are **`Avicia [AVO]`** and **`Nefarious Ravens`**.

**The Avicia finding is the big one.** Avicia appears as a *Sequoia ally* in a 2026 fixture — the same two guilds our map data says fought a war in 2023. If corroborated, that is a full arc: war (2023) → alliance (2026).

**Corroboration of every name above via API v3, 3 Sep 2026** (all real, all live):
- Silk Road [SILK] — created **2026-04-25**, 9 members, 237 wars, owner J4D8. A brand-new micro-guild; dates the fixture to mid-2026.
- Anime Lovers — now tagged **[Zamn]**, created 2022-08-01, 90 members, 5,083 wars, owner EXP. (Chief **karapandza** is the same guest writer who appeared in Titan Times v10–v11.)
- Chiefs Of Corkus [HOC] — created 2020-12-02, 110 members, 32,853 wars, owner Amber_Rocks_3.
- Radiant Roses [RARO] — created **2025-08-20**, 90 members, 10,589 wars, owner RealBlackRose.
- Nefarious Ravens — real tag is **[NFR]**, created 2020-03-10, 47,870 lifetime wars, but only **17 members and 0 territories** in 2026. Consistent with Titan Times v9 (25 Mar 2023) recording NFR's disbandment and land redistribution; NFR survives as a shell. Caveat: the fixture writes its tag as `NRA`, which is wrong — treat the *tags* in these fixtures as illustrative and the *names* as captured.

## 6. The only war narrative of the era — the Long Raid, Aug 2024

Four wars in our map data have no written account. Exactly one has any narrative source at all.

- **[ATTESTED]** Thread 318070, **Defervesco**, **9 Aug 2024**: *"Aequitas's claim in sky was under siege for some time last week. Enjoy this retelling of the war!"* One reply (PlasmaWarrior: *"Nice music choice."*).
- **[ATTESTED]** The video: **"Wynncraft Warring - The Long Raid"**, YouTube `RLRh14ns6ok`, channel **Frank Wynncraft** (UCTVz1eJ1xKEnlAITgsioL3g), published **4 Aug 2024**. Description in full: *"Join Aeq for the small guild warring experience!"*
- Therefore: **Frank Wynncraft is/was an Aequitas member**, and the video is an Aequitas-side account of a **multi-day siege of Aequitas's Sky Province claim, roughly 28 Jul – 4 Aug 2024**.
- **The attacker is never named** in the thread, the description, or the indexed page text. The only way to identify them is the video's own footage or our map DB.
- **[INFERENCE — flag for map cross-check]** The siege falls inside **Season 19 (24 Jun – 26 Aug 2024)**, which **Sequoia won with 13,480,252 SR — the highest season rating ever recorded**. An attacker capable of a week-long siege of a top-3 guild during Sequoia's record-breaking season is most likely Sequoia. *Do not publish this without checking the exchange data for Sky-province captures in that window.*
