## Two things the image settles that the text could not

The image gives the Coalition split as **16 February 2018** where the Google-Doc
timeline gives the 17th, which is exactly the disagreement Alliance Alliance
already records as "17 February 2018 (16 February in some accounts)".

It also dates Ordo Malleus' fall to **9 March 2016**, agreeing with the wiki
against the Google Doc's 11 March.
