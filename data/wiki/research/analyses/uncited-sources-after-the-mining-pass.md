# The archived sources still cited by nothing, and why

A mining pass over the 119 archived sources that no article cited brought that
count to 58. This note records what is left and the reason in each case, so the
next pass does not re-read the same dead ends.

## Recruitment threads: 44 of the 58

| Thread | Pages left | What they are |
|---|---|---|
| 119596 (Libertas) | 15 | Applications, 2016–17 |
| 278123 (Empire of Nemract) | 15 | Applications, 2020–23 |
| 255067 (The Simple Ones) | 6 | Applications, 2020 onward |
| 280525 (Aequitas) | 4 | Applications, 2021 |
| 278112 (the recruiting megalist) | 4 | Guild listings already covered by the cited pages |

These pages were read. They contain almost nothing about guild politics and a
great deal that must never be published: ages, countries, Skype handles, Discord
tags, school and work schedules, and in several cases health information
volunteered by applicants. What is usable from each thread came from its
**opening post** and from a handful of officers' replies, and those are cited:

- Empire of Nemract — the opening post in its 2020, 2021 and 2023 states, and
  the reopening of the thread in January 2023. Pages 2–18 are applications and
  the word "bump". Nothing was taken from them.
- Libertas — one page carries "as we got the guild back, we are only recruiting
  70+" (19 June 2016), which would date a recovery of the guild, and one gives
  the guild's weekday activity as two or three players. Neither was strong
  enough to build a sentence on, and taking them would have meant citing pages
  that are otherwise wall-to-wall personal data.
- The Simple Ones — the divisions, the Admiralty formula and the trial
  citizenship are now in the article, cited to pages 2, 3, 4 and 8. Pages 5–7
  and 9–11 are applications and long stretches of *Blackadder* script pasted as
  bumps.
- Aequitas — every remaining page is the same four-question form and the same
  acceptance message. The only fact in them is that warring was asked of
  applicants at level 95 and above, which the article already has.

**Rule this establishes:** a recruitment thread's value is concentrated in its
opening post and its officers' announcements. Mining its application pages costs
hours and returns nothing publishable.

## Two sources that were cited but whose limits should be remembered

- **`gdoc-1wlfun90rphwpjoy`** is an earlier draft of the Holders of LE timeline
  in `gdoc-1bbnalbfij8cjnog` and stops in October 2016. Cite the longer one
  except where the drafts differ.
- **`sequoia-ooo-index-wb20240322`** and **`-wb20250209`** are captures of the
  same page eleven months apart, and the page never got past its template text.
  They establish only that the site existed and did not change.

## Addendum, 4 September 2026: the ingested Discord channels

Twelve channels were archived from four guilds and alliances that day. Eleven
are cited. The exception is `coalition-public-announcements`: 169 words, one
post, about who could do what after a server rebuild. There is nothing in it
about guild history and it is not expected to be cited.

The rest were mined at very different depths, which is worth recording because
words-per-citation is the honest measure of how much is left:

| Source | Words | Citations | Words per citation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| emperium-voting | 2,825 | 3 | 942 |
| emperium-announcements | 19,605 | 24 | 817 |
| coalition-welcome | 326 | 1 | 326 |
| coalition-announcements | 1,333 | 6 | 222 |
| kingdomphoenixes-announcements | 2,465 | 14 | 176 |
| holders-of-le-announcements | 2,141 | 14 | 153 |

The corpus writes about thirty words of article per citation, so these numbers
are not comparable to that — a channel is raw material, and most of any
announcement channel is scheduling, promotions and jokes. But the Emperium's
two channels are the ones with real depth left. The announcements were read
through the June 2017 split debate; from late June to the October
fall they have been skimmed, not mined. The voting channel has been used for
its mechanics and not for its individual votes, of which there are dozens, each
one a dated decision by a named alliance.
