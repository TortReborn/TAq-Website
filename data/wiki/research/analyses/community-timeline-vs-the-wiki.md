# Reading the archived community timeline against the wiki

The community guild-history timeline was cited across the corpus for a long
time before a copy was held. Now that `community-guild-timeline` is an archived
Google Doc, its text can be read against the articles citing it.

An earlier version of this note claimed three contradictions. Checking each
against the articles rather than against memory, **two of the three were my
error and are retracted below.** They are left here rather than deleted,
because a retraction someone can read is more useful than a note that quietly
changes its mind.

## 1. Who declared war on 21 December 2017 — not a contradiction

The timeline's Dec 2017 entry reads:

> 21st Dec, War Syndicate declares war on the 1st Coalition.

This looked like a reversal of the wiki, which has the Coalition invading that
day. It is not. The War Syndicate page already explains the mechanism: the
Coalition read a line in a Discord exchange — "here we come" — as War
Syndicate's declaration, and invaded in response. A source naming War Syndicate
as the declaring party and a source describing the Coalition attacking after
being declared upon are the same story from two ends. Nothing to reconcile.

## 2. The date of the Coalition split — retracted, the wiki already carries both

> 17th Feb, HackForums [Hax], Paladins United [PUN], House of Sentinels [Snt],
> Emorians [ERN] and Kangronomicon [Fuq] leave the 1st Coalition.

I recorded this as a one-day disagreement the wiki had missed. The wiki has it.
Alliance Alliance opens "On 17 February 2018 (16 February in some accounts)",
and the Coalition Civil War page names which source gives which date. There was
nothing to fix.

## 3. "Alliance Alliance" as a name for the opposing bloc — retracted, two
different blocs

> They form Alliance Alliance with Fantasy [Fuq] and Necromonicon [Feq] and
> declare war on the 1st Coalition, beginning the Coalition Civil War.

I read this as supplying a name for the bloc the Federation's page calls
"otherwise unrecorded" — and conflated two alliances a year apart. Alliance
Alliance is the **February 2018** breakaway, and the wiki has a full page on
it. **[Ent]** is the tag of the **November 2018** enemy alliance during the
Federation's collapse, a different bloc entirely, and it is still true that no
other identifier for it survives.

## The one real finding: a citation id doing two jobs

Several War Syndicate quotations cite `community-guild-timeline` with the
locator "Community guild-timeline image (imgur seklYAQ)". Those words are in an
*image* of a timeline; the id now resolves to the Google Doc, which does not
contain them. A reader following the footnote lands on a document that does not
say what the footnote says it says.

The two artefacts also disagree with each other — the image gives the Coalition
split as 16 February, the document as 17 — which is precisely why they cannot
share an id. They need separate ids, and the quotations reassigned to whichever
artefact each came from.
