# What the archived community timeline says that the wiki does not

The community guild-history timeline was cited across the corpus for a long
time before a copy of it was held; the archive note on the source says so. Now
that `community-guild-timeline` is an archived Google Doc, its text can be read
against the articles that cite it, and three things do not line up. None is
settled here — each is a question for someone who was there.

## 1. Who declared war on 21 December 2017

The timeline's Dec 2017 entry reads:

> 21st Dec, War Syndicate declares war on the 1st Coalition.

The wiki has the Coalition attacking on that date, following Drew1011's
account, and the Titans Valor guild history agrees with the wiki's direction
("the Coalition alliance would declare war on December 21st"). The timeline is
alone in reversing it, and it is a secondary compilation, so this is weak
evidence against two better-placed sources — but it is not nothing, and the
wiki should not present the direction of the declaration as undisputed.

## 2. The date of the Coalition split

> 17th Feb, HackForums [Hax], Paladins United [PUN], House of Sentinels [Snt],
> Emorians [ERN] and Kangronomicon [Fuq] leave the 1st Coalition.

The wiki dates this to 16 February 2018 throughout. The five names match
exactly; only the day differs, by one.

## 3. The opposing bloc had a name

The Federation's page says the tag **[Ent]** "is the only identifier ever given
for the opposing bloc, which is otherwise unrecorded". The timeline names it:

> They form Alliance Alliance with Fantasy [Fuq] and Necromonicon [Feq] and
> declare war on the 1st Coalition, beginning the Coalition Civil War.

Five leavers plus Fantasy and Necronomicon is the same seven-guild bloc the
wiki describes, so "otherwise unrecorded" is no longer true. It also explains
the abbreviation **AA** in the timeline's December entries, which the War
Syndicate page quotes without expanding.

## A citation-hygiene note

Several War Syndicate quotations cite `community-guild-timeline` with the
locator "Community guild-timeline image (imgur seklYAQ)". Those words are in an
*image* of a timeline, not in the Google Doc that the citation id now resolves
to — a reader following the footnote lands on a document that does not contain
the quoted text. The two artefacts need separate ids.
