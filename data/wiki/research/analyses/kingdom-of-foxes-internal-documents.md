# The Kingdom of Foxes' internal documents, and where they disagree

Six previously unmined archived documents describe the Kingdom of Foxes, its
alliance and its offshoots from the inside:

| id | what it is |
|---|---|
| `gdoc-1kajulcrdc0cpf1j` | "Guild History" — a month-by-month table, Apr 2014 – Apr 2021, with Monarch / Viceroy / Government / Events columns. Compiled after the fact. |
| `gdoc-1b8e1nh_ate9h69n` | Member Handbook — rules, ranks, State Council, titles, decorations, war policy, alliance, and two timelines. Written c. Feb 2017. |
| `gdoc-1iacdk2t_3tzwo_a` | Constitution of Valkyrie, fourth edition. Undated; postdates the March 2016 admissions it records. |
| `gdoc-1bbnalbfij8cjnog` | Holders of LE owner / alliance / events timeline, Dec 2014 – Aug 2018. |
| `gdoc-1wlfun90rphwpjoy` | An earlier, shorter draft of the same Holders of LE timeline, ending Oct 2016. Two Owner cells differ: the later draft credits "Seb & Yos" and "Spham & Drew" where the earlier gives only "Seb" and "Spham". |
| `gdoc-1v5kpnrdnydscdrz`, `gdoc-1m7ns_yvlupswxmr`, `gdoc-1q2iebjuyn91dxov` | Kingdom of Phoenixes history, chronology and rank structure. |

## Tables whose cells did not survive the export

**`gdoc-1iacdk2t_3tzwo_a`, Part III (3.1) Table of Policies.** The raw capture is
a plain-text Google Docs export, not HTML, so there is no markup to recover: the
row labels (Vote in the Directorate, Permanent Membership, May Be Kicked Out,
Shared Borders, per-guild chat access, and so on) and the five column headings
survive, and every cell is empty. **Nothing may be cited about which policy
applied to which guild.** What is citable is the column list itself — Fox, Imp,
LTH, HHU, HoL — which is the largest membership any Valkyrie document names, and
which is used in the article for exactly that.

**`gdoc-1b8e1nh_ate9h69n`, Sections X and XI.** The raw capture *is* HTML and the
tables parse. Google Docs omits repeated empty cells, so a row with fewer cells
than the table has columns cannot be aligned. Only complete rows are safe:

- Historical Timeline: Jul 2016 (LoveLusting / Kurumi), Dec 2016 (Drew1011 &
  Teumessi / Killerfish / council I), Feb 2017 (Drew1011 / InvertedReaper /
  council VII), and the LoveLusting / Drew1011 / council VIII row.
- State Council Timeline: **only government I** (3 Dec 2016) is a complete
  nine-cell row — Chancellor Drew1011, Secretary Teumessi, Justiciar Killerfish,
  Advisor 01100110111, Designer LoveLusting, War Commander and Treasurer vacant.
  Governments II–IX have collapsed cells and their name-to-portfolio mapping is
  **not recoverable**; the article cites only their numbering and dates, which are
  the first two cells of every row and are unambiguous.

The same document also fixes that Pyrias, "Misty" and Teumessi are one person:
it has Pyrias found the House of Sentinels on 2 July 2016 and, in November,
"Pyrias, newly risen as Teumessi", and then has Queen Teumessi step down on the
date Drew1011 gives for Misty stepping down.

## Disagreements now in the articles

- **HaHaUnited's Valkyrie entry.** 18 March 2016 in the Valkyrie constitution,
  the community timeline and the Guild History; 20 May 2016 in the Fox handbook.
  Both are stated in [[valkyrie]].
- **Holders of LE and Kangronomicon.** Drew1011 gives 14 August 2015 for the move
  to subguild status; the Holders of LE timeline gives 15 August, and has the
  guild back inside Concordia from 24 August until 22 September. Both in
  [[holders-of-le]].
- **The Kingdom of Phoenixes' November Exodus.** One Phoenix document dates it 3
  November 2017 and has members leaving voluntarily after failing to force
  AurumKitsune's resignation, then founding the Serpentem Empire [SNK]; the other
  dates it 4 November, names Whimsicality, ElegantDeath and Alhecks, and records
  them as expelled for high treason. Both in [[kingdomphoenixes]].
- **Foxton Legacy's creation.** 24 November 2016 in Drew1011's account and in the
  handbook, 25 November in the Guild History. The handbook reconciles them: the
  guild was created on the 24th and declared as [Eon] on the 25th.

## Disagreements deliberately left out of the articles

- **The collapse of Ordo Malleus.** The wiki dates it 9 March 2016, following
  Drew1011, who withdrew Holders of LE that day. The Holders of LE timeline
  separates the two: HoL withdrew on 9 March and the alliance "collapses as more
  guilds leave" on 10 March. One day, and the two are compatible readings of the
  same week, so nothing was changed.
- **The Council of Canyon Kingdoms' founding.** The chronicle database gives 15
  September 2019 and is ground truth; the Fox Guild History gives 18 September.
  The database was not changed, and the article keeps 15 September.
- **The guild-defence rework of March 2018.** The Fox Guild History dates the
  buff to 1 March 2018 and the Federation's recovery to 5 March; the Holders of
  LE timeline gives 2 March and 5 March. Not used either way.

## What is not cited anywhere, and why

`gdoc-1zch0qk3lwi8ho5p` is a spreadsheet counting members by title — Page,
Squire, Sir/Madame, Lord/Lady, Baron, Viscount, Count/Countess, Marquess, Duke,
Viceroy, Royalty — across thirty dates from 2 April 2017 to 8 September 2018. It
carries no title, author or guild name.

It is very probably a Kingdom of Foxes census: the Viceroy count is 1 throughout,
matching Drew1011's unbroken tenure from 7 February 2017 to 7 February 2019, and
the Royalty count rises from 2 to 3 in the row dated 27 February 2018, five days
after the Guild History has Snowing re-established as Queen Consort. But the
title set is not the handbook's — it has Page and Squire, which the Fox handbook
does not, and lacks the handbook's Dame and Knight — so the identification rests
on two coincidences and is not strong enough to cite. If a source is ever found
that ties the sheet to a guild, it becomes a month-by-month roster of the Fox
nobility across the Federation year, which nothing else in the archive provides.
