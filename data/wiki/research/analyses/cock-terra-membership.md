# The Council of Canyon Kingdoms was not "the victorious Terra bloc"

Recorded 4 Sep 2026, after an expert reader of the period flagged it. Corrected
in the article and in the chronicle database. Written down so it is not
reintroduced by a later drafting pass reaching for a tidy phrase.

## The claim that was wrong

The article said the Council was "formed on 15 Sep 2019 by seven guilds of the
victorious Terra bloc", listing Kingdom Foxes, DiamondDeities, Titans Valor,
The Simple Ones, Fantasy, Phantom Hearts and Empire of Sindria.

The list is correct and sourced. The characterisation is false, and the source
cited for it says the opposite a few lines further down.

## What the evidence says

**Two of the seven were never in Terra.** The chronicle database's Terra roster
is Phantom Hearts, The Simple Ones, DiamondDeities, TheNoLifes, Lunatic,
IceBlue Team, Paladins United, Fantasy, Achte Shadow, FinalFront, BlueStoneGroup
and Titans Valor — twelve guilds, including neither **Kingdom Foxes** nor
**Empire of Sindria**. Five of the Council's seven founders were Terra guilds.

**Kingdom Foxes was on the other side.** The community timeline records it
co-founding **Luna** on 14 Feb 2019, and leaving Luna on **18 Sep 2019** — three
days *after* the Council was founded. At the moment of founding, one of the
seven was still a member of the alliance Terra had just beaten.

**The cited source carries no Terra roster at all.** Its entry for Terra's
formation reads "Terra is formed by: (placeholder)". The phrase could not have
been drawn from it, however the citation looked.

## Why it survived

The citation was real and the list it supported was accurate, so the sentence
read as sourced. Only the adjectival clause was invented. That is the failure
mode to watch for: **a true, well-cited fact with an unsourced characterisation
welded onto it**, where the footnote appears to cover the whole sentence.

## What else was wrong in the same article

Checking this exposed four dates, each exactly one day early against the
timeline they cite: TheNoLifes joining (28 → 29 Sep), BlueStoneGroup (10 → 11
Oct), Vindicator (14 → 15 Oct) and Kingdom Foxes departing (1 → 2 Nov). The
last had been narrativised — Fox's departure was called "the eve of Terra's
formal fall", a phrase built to fit the wrong date. Fox left on the 2nd, the
same day Terra fell.

The drift is local to this article, not a systematic import error: the
corpus-wide date check shows no single dominant shift.

## Standing rule

It is acceptable not to know something. It is not acceptable to state something
the sources do not support, or that they contradict. Where a bloc's composition
matters, cite the roster; where no roster survives, say so.
