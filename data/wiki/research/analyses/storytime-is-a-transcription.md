# The storytime document is a transcription, and four lines diverge from the original

**Status: established. Treat `drew1011-storytime` as a transcription, not as the
posted text.**

## What it is

`data/wiki/sources/docs/drew1011-storytime.md` is a transcription of a serialised
account posted to a guild Discord channel in 2018. Its own header says so: no
public URL, posted to a private channel. The original messages exist in the
offline archive that the `-discord-private` sources cite.

## What diverges

A line-by-line comparison of the transcription against the original messages —
2,454 posted messages against 1,076 substantive lines here — found 1,684 exact
matches and fourteen divergences. Ten are line-splitting artefacts. Four change
content, and none of the four original messages carries an edit timestamp, so
none of them was altered after posting:

| Original | This document | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| a ceremony in **Cinfras** | a ceremony in **Llevigar** | changes where the March 2016 Valkyrie admission happened |
| as of **this** month | as of **last** month | changes when |
| someone named **"Arenos"** | someone named **"PaladinArenos"** | normalises a handle and drops the quotation marks showing the narrator did not yet know who he was |
| my entire guild **career** | my entire guild **history** | stylistic |

That is a light editorial pass rather than corruption. But two of the four change
a fact, and this document is cited nineteen times across ten articles as
first-person testimony.

## What follows for editors

- **Do not quote this document as verbatim text on a load-bearing sentence.**
  Where the exact wording matters, paraphrase, or have the line checked.
- **Any single line can be settled against the original in under a minute** by
  asking the research side for it by line number. That is cheaper than
  re-transcribing the file and is the standing arrangement.
- The Cinfras error was corrected on `valkyrie`, `holders-of-le` and
  `guild-community-and-institutions` in September 2026; the quotation on
  `valkyrie` became a paraphrase, because a quotation of a transcription cannot
  promise what quotation marks promise.
- The other two testimony documents, `cameron-oral-history` and
  `thundderr-oral-history`, have not been compared against anything and may be
  transcriptions on the same footing.
