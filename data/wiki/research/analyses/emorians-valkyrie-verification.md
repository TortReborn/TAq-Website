# Verification: "Emorians [ERN] re-joined Valkyrie ~2021"

Claim source: Peng (Emorians-era veteran), relayed 2026-09-02.
Verified: 2026-09-02.

## VERDICT

**NOT FOUND IN WRITING — and mild affirmative counter-evidence exists.**

No document from 2020–2023 places Emorians inside the Valkyrie alliance. Every
source in that window that enumerates Valkyrie's membership names only **Kingdom
Foxes, Imperial, and Paladins United**. Two independent 2021 posts point the other
way (details in §A and §B below).

This is *not* a positive disproof — Emorians' own guild thread never listed
alliances at all, so the PUN-style attestation method has no purchase on ERN, and
a community-alliance membership could in principle exist unrecorded. But the claim
cannot be entered into the record as attested.

**Recommendation:** keep the existing record unchanged (single Valkyrie stint,
22 May 2018 → 28 Aug 2019). If Peng's testimony is logged, log it as
uncorroborated oral testimony with a note that the contemporaneous written record
contradicts it at two points.

## Tightest date bracket

- **No attestation at any date, 2020–2023.**
- Negative coverage is continuous over **25 May 2021 → 15 Sep 2022** (the span of
  sources that actually enumerate Valkyrie's roster or ERN's alliances).
- The narrowest window in which an unattested second stint could hide:
  **after 6 Jun 2021** (last post enumerating ERN's alliances, which gives Hestia
  only) and ending before or after the Sep 2022 Valkyrie dissolution — the ERN
  thread captures of Nov 2021 / Jan 2022 / May 2022 cannot exclude it because
  that thread never carried an alliance section in any revision.
- **End date: not recoverable** (no start date to bracket from).

## A. Counter-evidence 1 — Valkyrie's roster stated unchanged, May 2021

Thread 278112 (megalist), page-7, Wayback capture **20211128211949**.
Post by **Dream (Dream96), May 25, 2021**, replying to Sg_Voltage asking which
alliances had renamed:

> "Valhalla and Kaos (Khaos?), Valkyrie stayed the same iirc. The rest of the
> minor alliances I dont really know"

Immediately preceded (same page) by the prompting post:

> "The alliance collumn is outdated and needs a major revamp. Its probably better
> to empty it out for now and let people edit in their own alliance again, since
> the two major alliances both disbanded and got new names."

Read: as of late May 2021, in an explicit audit of which alliances had changed,
Valkyrie is singled out as the one that had *not* changed. An ERN re-join in or
before May 2021 would have been a change.

## B. Counter-evidence 2 — ERN's alliances enumerated Jun 2021, Valkyrie absent

Same thread 278112, page-7, capture **20211128211949**.
Post by **Pally, Jun 6, 2021, post #140**:

> "idk if you're putting multiple alliances, but you can add Hestia to Titans
> Valor [ANO], Emorians [ERN], Lux Nova [LXA], Empire of Sindria [ESI], Paladins
> United [PUN], and Hackforums [Hax]

This is the megalist post already on record establishing Hestia. Note the phrasing
"you can **add** Hestia to..." — the poster is appending to whatever the sheet
already held, so it is not a full enumeration and does **not** by itself exclude a
concurrent Valkyrie entry for ERN. Weight it as suggestive, not conclusive.

## J. Wattpad guild history — corroborates the FIRST stint only

"Inside Myself — Recent Wynncraft Guild History", wattpad.com/784189853.
Surfaced by search as a Valkyrie/Emorians hit; on reading, it is a **2019**
account (self-dated "September 14th"), describing the events already on record:

> "The Valkyrie alliance, consisting of Kingdom Foxes, Imperial, Paladins United,
> and Emorians, have existed for longer than I've played Wynncraft."

> "The raid, lasting only two days, ended in utter failure due to the Valkyrie
> alliance's controversial use of sub guilds."

> "For over a month, the Valkyrie alliance, now calling themselves the Luna
> alliance, retained map dominance."

> "All of a sudden, the Emorians, supposedly frustrated with the actions of other
> prominent guilds, left the Luna alliance."

This is the 28 Aug 2019 revolt, matching the existing record exactly (ERN in
Valkyrie → Valkyrie renames to Luna → ERN leaves). **It is about the first stint
and must not be mistaken for evidence of a second.** Note: AI search summaries
readily present this passage's "Valkyrie... and Emorians" roster stripped of its
2019 dating — a trap worth flagging for future researchers.
