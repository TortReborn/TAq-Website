# The [Aeu] tag, and why ILQ's 2020 roster is not evidence of its alliances

Compiled 4 Sep 2026 from the round-3 read of threads 255067 (The Simple Ones)
and 278123 (Empire of Nemract). Both questions below are **open**; neither is
asserted in article prose. This note exists so they are not re-proposed as
discoveries.

## 1. Two guilds hold the [Aeu] tag, three years apart

| Source | Date | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| thread 255067, OP as last edited **16 Sep 2020** | 2020 | "[ILQ] The Simple Ones … was eventually merged with XeraAndromeda's **[Aeu] The Aetherius** on February the 8th" of 2019 |
| thread 278123, OP as last edited **18 Apr 2023** | 2023 | Member lists link **The Simple Ones** and **Admiralia**; the Empire is branded "ILQ + Aeu" |
| community guild-timeline (already in the chronicle) | — | The Empire of Nemract was formed 17 Aug 2020 by The Simple Ones and **Mystic Woods** |

So the Aeu tag belongs to **The Aetherius** in the 2020 reading and to
**Admiralia** in the 2023 one, while the timeline names a third guild,
**Mystic Woods**, as the Empire's other half.

**The tempting inference — that these are one guild renamed twice — is not
supported.** Nothing in the archive links the three names, "The Aetherius"
appears in exactly one source (thread 255067) and "Admiralia" in one other, and
a tag freed by a merger can be claimed by an unrelated guild. Equally, the 2019
merger and the 2020 union are different kinds of event: a merger absorbs a
guild, and the Empire had two member guilds, so The Aetherius cannot simply have
become the Empire's second half without something reviving it.

**What the articles say:** the founding merger with The Aetherius (Simple Ones),
and the Admiralia member lists with the "renamed or replaced, never recorded"
caveat (Empire of Nemract). Neither claims the identification.

**What would settle it:** any source naming two of the three together, a
Wynncraft API record for Admiralia or The Aetherius carrying a creation date, or
any dated use of [Aeu] between Feb 2019 and Nov 2020.

## 2. ILQ's Sep 2020 roster lists an alliance that had already collapsed

The same OP says "ILQ is a member of the CoCK or Council of Canyon Kingdoms
alliance." The chronicle database ends that membership on **23 Nov 2019**, when
the alliance collapsed — ten months before the OP's last edit.

This is **not** a correction to the database. Forum opening posts are edited in
place, and this one was plainly updated in sections: its roster carries the
Empire-era imperial titles (Sep 2020) while its "Foreign Relations" block still
carries 2019 alliances. An edit date is the date of the *last* edit to any part
of the post, and says nothing about when a given line was written.

**Rule this establishes for the whole corpus:** a quoted or last-edited opening
post dates its *newest* element only. Use it for facts that can only have been
written recently (a roster naming an office that did not exist earlier, a
requirement that only tightens); do not read an undated line in it as current at
the edit date. The one alliance taken from this roster into the article, the
Wynnic Union, is stated as a claim the roster makes, not as a dated membership.
