# Federation departures with no announcement (capture-log analysis, 3 Sep 2026)

**Question.** The Federation's announcement channel names most joins and departures,
but several guilds simply stop appearing in the territory-assignment lists with no
announcement at all. The channel dossier proposed inferred end dates for nine of
them. Inferred dates are the weakest thing this wiki publishes, so each was checked
against `territory_exchanges` before anything was written.

**Method.** For each guild, every exchange in 2018 was bucketed by month and split
two ways: taken versus lost, and against a Federation member versus an outsider,
using the chronicle's own membership stints to decide who was a member on the day.

The signal that matters: **a guild inside the alliance shows high-volume, almost
perfectly symmetric exchange with fellow members** — that is the free-for-all
trading pattern the methodology notes describe, not war. A guild that has left
either goes quiet or starts trading blows asymmetrically with outsiders.

## Confirmed, and applied

| Guild | Was | Now | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deimos | left 10 Nov | **31 Mar 2018** | No assignment after 21 Feb; every capture in its record stops on 31 March. |
| Immortalish | left 10 Nov | **31 Mar 2018** | Same absence; activity collapses in March, one stray exchange afterwards. |
| Holders of LE | left 10 Nov | **4 Jun 2018** | Assigned 24 territories on 10 May and none on 4 Jun; captures stop after May. |
| LE Flowers | left 15 Mar | **24 Mar 2018** | Still carried an assigned region on 24 March, after its recorded departure. |
| As Darkness Falls | joined 16 Feb | **10 Mar 2018** | Absent from the founding division, first assigned 10 Mar — and its exchanges run against outsiders through February, turning to internal trading only in March. |

## Refuted by the data, and left alone

- **BuildCraftia** — proposed end ~30 March, on the strength of a storytime expulsion
  vote. The log says otherwise: from April to October it exchanges 1,000–2,300
  territories a month with Federation members at near-perfect symmetry (1,157 taken
  against 1,158 lost in April; 94–100% of its activity against members), and the
  pattern only breaks in November when the alliance dissolves. That is a member, not
  an expelled guild. Its recorded 10 November end stands.
- **TheEnchanted** — proposed end ~March. It stays active through 24 October with a
  high share against members, and its recorded end of 24 October is exactly its last
  exchange. Already correct.
- **Emorians** — proposed a later join, since it is absent from the 24 March
  assignment. But its February activity at 54% against Federation members is the
  Coalition Civil War, not membership: Emorians was in Alliance Alliance and fighting
  them. The recorded 20 March join stands.
- **Illustratus** — activity in February and March, a gap through midsummer, scraps in
  August and September, and its last exchanges in November against non-members.
  Nothing here is decisive either way, so the recorded end is left as it is and the
  case is noted as unresolved rather than given a manufactured date.

## What this exercise shows

Five of nine proposals held; four did not, and one of the four was refuted by a
wide margin. The proposals were reasonable readings of the announcement channel —
a guild vanishing from the assignment lists really does suggest it left — but the
assignment lists record *administrative* allocation, and a guild can keep playing
inside an alliance without appearing on them.

The general point: **absence from a document is weak evidence, and the capture log
can usually turn it into a strong answer either way.** Inferences that survive this
check are worth publishing; the ones that do not are worth recording as refuted, so
nobody proposes them again.
