# The Kingdom of Phoenixes' two internal records, and where they disagree

Two documents from inside the guild are archived:

- `gdoc-1v5kpnrdnydscdrz`, "The History of the Kingdom of Phoenixes, From
  September 2017 to the Present" — a table of ruling monarch, serving viceroy,
  named government and events, running to 9 February 2018.
- `gdoc-1m7ns_yvlupswxmr`, a dated event log in the same guild's hand, running
  to 22 November 2017.

Both stop mid-record. Neither names an author.

## Where they agree

The founding on 23 September 2017 out of Kingdom Foxes; Drew1011 made viceroy
on 2 November in Alhecks' place; AurumKitsune stepping down on 22 November and
passing the crown to Thundderr. These are the spine of the guild's first
season and both documents carry them identically.

## Where they do not

The break that followed Drew1011's appointment is given twice, differently.

| | Date | Character | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Event log | 4 November 2017 | Whimsicality, ElegantDeath and Alhecks **removed** "for high treason, forever forbidden from coming back", having pressed the Queen to hand the guild over | three named officers |
| History table | 3 November 2017 | after failing to force the Queen's resignation, "many KoP members **leave**"; they found the Serpentem Empire | unnamed members |

One day apart, and an expulsion in one against a walkout in the other. The
articles give both and prefer neither. It is worth noting that a guild's
official log calling three officers traitors and its history calling the same
break a departure are exactly the two things a guild would write at different
distances from the event.

That disagreement is also the best evidence about what these documents are:
they are two compilations rather than one running log, since a single
contemporaneous record could not differ from itself.

## The tier question is still open

Both `gdoc-1m7ns_yvlupswxmr` and the Holders of LE ownership timelines
(`gdoc-1bbnalbfij8cjnog`, `gdoc-1wlfun90rphwpjoy`) carry the archive note "Tier
UNSET: whether this was kept as events happened or compiled afterwards is not
established, and that determines how much weight it carries. Establish before
citing." They are cited anyway — seventeen times between them before this pass,
more after it.

Nothing found so far settles it. The documents are undated and unsigned, and
their internal evidence cuts both ways: the level-and-star entries in the
Phoenix log ("the guild reached level 31 and the first star was obtained") read
like something written as it happened, while the phrase "forever forbidden from
coming back" reads like something written afterwards with feeling.

Anyone who was in the guild could close this in a sentence. Until someone does,
treat both as retrospective — the weaker reading — and prefer a forum post where
one exists.
