So currently the meta of being top 10 on the guild sr leaderboard is to join valhalla and laugh at other guilds.

Seriously tho guilds who are not in that alliance barely has a chance of making in to top 15 even. I think most of us have seen the difference between #14 and #15 the place last season.(#14 and up are the mega alliance guilds btw) Also attackers put 10x more effort in to warring and they only get a small fraction of rewards while the alliance members always get the top rewards. Here is my suggestion on how to change it.

Split the leaderboard in to attackers and defenders. lemme explain

For the defenders leaderboard guilds receive sr for holding territories so pretty much the current sr gaining way or even before 1.20.4 sr changes except guilds don't get sr for capturing a territory.(This is probably mostly just going to be for the mega alliance to gain rewards)

Now for attackers leaderboard. They receive sr for capturing territories.(This is for the none alliance members.)

And top guilds on both leaderboard should receive rewards on same value. (Like different skin crowns for #1, different banner structures etc.)

Now incase defenders aka alliance people would be able to hog both leaderboards then. Well to stop that i was thinking how about the longer a territory has been held by one guild more attackers leaderboard sr will be received for the guild who captured that territory. Now its obvious that the longer territory holds will be from the val guild territories so.......

1)The alliance members can either not care about this attacker leaderboard and just work on defenders leaderboard

2)They can poke at alliance member territories for attacker lb sr which will most likely likely lead to the whole map turning into an ffa somehow.

I feel like if this were to happen attackers/raiders will get more motivated and they raiding val guilds actually have some use from it. also they actually have a chance to get top rewards now.
