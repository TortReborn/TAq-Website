That isn't the definition of subguilding, nor is subguilding used to "keep the amount of your guild on check." Subguilding is a war-related technique involving the use of secondary guilds to increase the offensive or defensive capability of a main guild.

It's worth noting that many "small guilds" are generally content with their size since they are usually comprised of an IRL friend group or similar and have no intention of growing like Wynncraftia does.

Any guild that genuinely wishes to grow would find your offer rather rude since you're essentially asking them to abandon their own guild and be subordinate to yours, especially when your own guild has yet to make a name for itself or achieve anything significant.

Your idea is good on paper but lacks an incentive to be followed through in my opinion.
