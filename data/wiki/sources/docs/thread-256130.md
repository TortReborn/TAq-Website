Guild community is actually more complex than most people look upon it as, it keeps the community alive. Sadly at that cost there is "beef" between different guilds and entire different alliances which lead to situations where "hate" fuels wars between guilds. In the most basic sense, there are guilds which have "claims" which are their areas on the map, like, ANO has Jungle, Nesaak, and mine base plains, Fox has a claim over northern and mid canyon. These claims can range from small like only 10 territories in the case of a guild like Phi or huge in terms of Fox or Imperial, these territories pump emeralds and exp into your guild, so the guild community came up with an unwritten rule, which is that if you attack a claim of a guild, they will retaliate, knowing this people harbor extreme senses of "patriotism and nationalism" for their guild, which can lead to attacks using hate.

### post #12 — IceResistance (Titans Valor [ANO] Founder) — Dec 12, 2019 at 7:42 PM

While we have our own individual guild communities, it is principal that we all communicate to some degree and intertwine. Together, we form the guild community as we know it, which is the greater whole.

Often there are some grievances and other tensions between guilds, but we have to realize we have a lot more in common then differences. The fact that we are all present in this community already shows a lot, how we all have this in common and it is something we should celebrate.

Essentially, I believe in order to eliminate tensions within the community, a line has to be drawn and made clear between war and community relations. What occurs in war often leaves a lasting impact and always finds it way meddling in our community relations. Ultimately, we need to begin to separate war and is its own function and not let it disrupt the camaraderie of the community as a whole.

In short, wars do not have to define our community relationships with each other.
