All Currently Recruiting Guilds Megalist

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------​

TLDR Here's the link; Google Sheets

Looking to join a guild but can't be bothered to look over 30+ threads to find the right one for you? Then this is the thread for you!

I've assembled a list of every* guild which is currently recruiting along with some important stats for each, such as the type of guild, their guild level and your level requirement, along with a link to each recruiting thread so you can quickly access it without needing to search the forums for it. My goal is to be able to make sure every guild gets the chance to shine and every player can get the chance to find the guild that's right for them. Ideally, all this information would be available in game, but since that isn't how it is, this is the next best option. Please keep in mind not all the information on the spreadsheet is perfectly up to date, the best source of information will always be the guilds recruitment thread.

If you're a high ranking member of a guild and want to see your guild added or want to make changes, go to the bottom for more details. Please keep in mind in order for a guild to be listed it must be at least level 35 (Age of guild is irrelevant). Also keep in mind that while this list is periodically updated by me, if you want something updated it is your responsibility to either notify me (message or comment are fine) or make the change yourself. Thanks for understanding :P

* * * ​

Here's a brief list of every piece of what's included on the list and what it means:

Recruiting - Is the guild looking for new members? Open guilds are taking applications from anyone who meets their requirements, If a guild becomes inactive or is no longer looking to recruit new members, it will be marked as Closed . Invitation means the guild will ask you to join them and See Thread means the guild has irregular recruitment policy (usually temporary membership). If you are an owner and have editing permission, please use this when you are no longer taking applications.

Guild Code and Name - The guilds 3 (or 4) letter code and name. You probably want to know the name of the guild you're applying for and it should make looking for more info on them using Wynncraft's website easier.

Language - The main language spoken by the guild. If you're a guild who are mostly one language but allow speakers of another language to join, you may want to include that in the additional notes section and if you only allow speakers of one language other then English that must be included in the additional requirements section.

Guild Level - Level of the guild. This is included to determine the listing order since it's more fair then alphabetical. If you want a smaller or newer guild your best bet is to look from the bottom up. Guild must reach level 35 in order to be added to this list.

Guild Size and Creation - How many people (on average) are in the guild and when the guild was made. Small is <20, Medium is 21-40 and large is 40+ members. If a guild is on the fence between two groups, it is marked as the smaller category. This section is a work in progress, it may change in the future.

Type - The primary (and secondary) purpose of a guild. Warring means lots of wars, Community means events outside of wars are planned and hosted, Trading is for guilds who run a guild shop, Roleplay is for guilds who roleplay, Event/Game is for guilds who run events outside of their guild (for non guild members), and Meme is for, well meme guilds and Other is for if you do something very specific which falls outside the scope of the previous 7. If you are an "other" or "event/game" guild, make sure to say why in the additional information section and/or let me know why.

Required Level - Most guilds require you to be a minimum level to apply, this is that level. Any means they're open to applications from anyone, however most guilds of that nature expect to see you increase your level over time, so keep that in mind when applying.

Additional Requirements - Some guilds require applicants to have met other requirements beyond level like having a Discord (very common), being above a certain age or having a minimum number of hours. This is where those requirements are listed. This section may also contain information about what the guild expects of you, such as xp or emerald contributions.

Additional Notes - This is for guilds to fill in themselves, can be a short message they want to tell people, can be a pitch, can be anything really. Most guilds won't have something here unless they asked for permission to edit the page.

Special Ranks - Some guilds have minor roleplay elements relating to ranks and they go beyond the usual five. If that's the case, they'll be marked as yes, to see what custom ranks they have, check their application page. These guilds tend to have more room for promotion and specialization if you're interested in that sort of thing.

Alliances - Some guilds are in alliances for warring, this is where they're listed. This should help you see what guilds are connected so you don't accidentally end up in a rival faction to a friend.

Thread Link - Thread links to the page where you can find more details and apply to the guild .

* * * ​

To Guild Owners/High Ranking Guild Members - If you're interested in editing your guilds info on this list or adding your guild to this list, send me a message with your Gmail and I'll give you editing permission, all I ask is that you only change your guilds information, are respectful of other guilds and follow the formatting. If you want any new columns, tags or anything else added, let me know so I can make the change. Thanks!
