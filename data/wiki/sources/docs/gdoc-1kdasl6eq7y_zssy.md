Every guild should have a chance at warring. Therefore, we won’t be attacking the mainland of Wynn, although the guilds within our alliance will stay maintain Gavel, and most of the islands.

* The 24 territory cap does not include FFA land. Every guild within or outside of the alliance can claim FFA territories without it counting towards the cap.
* We wish to prevent guilds taken over too much giving others guilds less of a chance. Thus, to make every guild have a possible shot, we won’t let any guild surpass 24 territories. As mentioned above, territories we count as FFA do not count towards this cap.
