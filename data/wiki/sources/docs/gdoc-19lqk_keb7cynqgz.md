This is The Economists’ Guild shop, where almost all of our members have a part in this shop!
Our end goal is to have a shop where the convenience of our customers and guild members is our utmost priority. We envision the largest shop in all of wynn, with a large customer flow, being able to connect adventurers to the items that they need within a single shop!


 4940224.png 

Join our guild discord at https://discord.gg/pFt7udH , There, in #marketplace, you can tag the guild member that has an item, and barter or buy for a price!


Alternatively, you can reply this this thread, and I will get back to you ASAP.


The layout is very straightforward, and works like almost every forum shop out there. Items are listed, and a price may or may not be included.


If an item does not include a price tag, please tag the person in discord, along with an offer!


If you have a specific item you’re looking for, try out ctrl + f or cmd + f for easy searching!
