2.2.2 ►

Version 2.2.1 is a minor update to Wynncraft . 2.2.1's major features are a new room to
The Wartorn Palace , new Lootrunning Missions and a new Trial, and allowing Guild Allies to raid together.

Version 2.2.1 was teased with a post by Wynncraft's official Twitter account, hinting at Guild Ally Raiding and removing a quest. A picture accompanied this tweet showing a scene from
The Missing Piece with Elise having a dialogue portrait. [ 1 ]

World Changes

- Remastered the following soundtracks in western Gavel : Epic Gavel ( Gavel Gate ), Monument of a New Land ( Llevigar ), Ten Minutes 'til Breaktime ( Karoc Quarry ), Gavel Journey ( Llevigar Plains ), Trodden-Down Paths ( Olux Swamp ), and Gray Skies, Dark Days ( Olux ).

- Added a new song for Bucie (Gallop Past the Riverside).

- Most mobs in the Nesaak Tundra and the Silent Expanse got new abilities.

- Some mobs in the Nesaak Tundra and the Silent Expanse got new models.

- Several mobs of
The Qira Hive 's Thunder Division got new abilities.

- Added a Wyvern free-fly zone in The Void .

- The Bucie Horse Merchant now sells Horses with 1/10/30 stats instead of 1/10/20.

- Adjusted the Lord Estalis boss's statue attack:
- The attack is now used when under 70% HP and has a cooldown between uses.

- The statues now rise out of the ground a little faster.

- The True Statue is easier to spot among the False Statues.

- Updated the builds around the Durum Isles .

- All Relic Guardians now have set spawn points and spawn consistently.

- Adjusted Syndra 's boss fight in
Echoes of Change :
- Reduced the time the fire AoE attacks remain in the arena when spawned.

- Removed knockback from the fire AoE attacks.

- The large fire now appears at 33% HP instead of 50% HP.

- Removed Remote Detonation Devices respawning in Dr. Legendary 's fight and drastically reduced their vision.

- Some windmills now use models instead of blocks.

- Increased the damage and HP of Cliff Scuttlers in the Bat Cave grind spot.

- Added a new
World Event in the Nesaak Tundra .

Raid Changes

- Removed elemental defenses from all Raid bosses, including Guild Raids.

- The blast ring from the volcanoes in the Guild Raid version of the Canyon Colossus 's boss fight is now red instead of gray.

-
The Wartorn Palace :
- Added a new Room 2 variant: Intruding Rifts

- Made the following changes to The Spire's Shadow's (Room 3A) Guild Raid version:
- The wielder's charge no longer collides with other players, but anchors still cannot be destroyed if they are being held by someone else.

- Anchors bury themselves in 2 seconds instead of 3 seconds when not held.

Lootrun Changes

- Readjusted Mythic drop chances in Lootruns.

- Adjusted reward pool levels across all Lootrun Camps to better reflect the region they are located in.

- Slightly increased the strength of unpowered Beacons, and slightly decreased the strength of powered Beacons.

- Beacon boosting is now additive instead of multiplicative.

- Rainbow Beacons only appear after at least 10 challenges were completed, gradually increasing the chance of them appearing until they show up.

- Added six new Lootrun Missions and a new Lootrun Trial.

- Adjusted several Missions and Trials:
- Thrill Seeker: Completely reworked
- Old: Red Beacons will reward 1 Boon out of 5.

- New: Gain +1 Pull per Red Beacon Challenge completed. This amount is increased by +1 per 7 challenges completed (max +3). This boost is reset upon taking a Green Beacon.

- Hoarder: Decreased required chests from 7 to 6 and the chosen Boon is now at 200% potency.

- Equilibrium: Potency boost increased from +50% to +100%, with the cap also increasing from +300% to +600%.

- Jester's Trick: Decreased required items from 30 to 25, 1-2 Curses can be acquired instead of only one, and +60s can be acquired instead of +30s.

- Chronotrigger: Green Beacons now cleanse 10% of your Curses instead of 15%, and pulls are boosted by 1% with a max of 5% instead of 3.5%.

- Adrenaline Junkie: The penalty is now -100% Boon Potency every 15s in interludes instead of losing a Boon every 30s in interludes.

- Warmth Devourer: 3 Challenges are consumed after finishing a challenge instead of 1 Boon and 1 Challenge.

Ability Changes

- Blanket buffed Mythic ultimate Aspects .

Warrior

- Added two new abilities:
- Final Crush:
- On page 3 at Quadruple Bash's former location, affiliated with the Fallen archetype.

- Your last Bash hit is delayed, but now has more area and deals greater damage.

- Concussion:
- On page 7, connected to Bloodlust.

- Final Crush deals extra damage to enemies damaged by the previous Bash hit.

- Merged Flyby Jab and Heavy Impact into Flyby Slam, being at Heavy Impact's former spot on the Ability Tree .

- Quadruple Bash is no longer associated with the Fallen archetype and is now at Flyby Jab's former spot.

- Boiling Blood now activates on the last Bash hit.

- Massive Bash now acts as a percentage increase based on the hit's size instead of direct addition.

- Reduced the Aspect of Empowering Fantasy's bonus Radiance duration from +2/3.5/4s to +1/1.65/2.

- Reduced the Aspect of Earthshaking's bonus Bash area of effect from +0.65/1.15/1.65/2 to +0.5/0.9/1.3/1.5.
