Idiot Co [ICo] was founded on November 26th, 2017 by DoomDeath1313. We are a level 105 guild that accepts all level 100+ players, no matter your class or favorite way to play the game - the only requirement, of course, is that you are an idiot like us!

Why Should I Join? ​

There are many perks to joining Idiot Co, some of which are listed here:

~ Weekly giveaways for LE, mythics, and more!

~ Opportunities to war to your heart's content, with rewards like guild tomes and emeralds for participating! We have 24/7 warring in FFAs and help people make/obtain war builds.

~ Equipment loaning (XP/GXP/LR sets, mythics, a lot of random items.)

~ Frequent community events & access to a wider multi-game community! We host xp grinding parties, dungeon run parties, carries for quests, and more!

~ Lessons on how to make money. You will be paid for warring, and I'm pretty sure there's no embezzling at all in this guild.

~ I mean, come on, we’re literally called Idiot Co!
