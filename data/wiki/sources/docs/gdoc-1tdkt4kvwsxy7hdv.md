Titans Valor [ANO]                                                              Kingdom of Foxes [Fox]
  

Non-Aggression Pact
During the duration of this non-aggression pact, both parties, Kingdom of Foxes, nor Titans Valor shall attack each other under any circumstance. They are obliged to be friendly towards each other, and respect lands owned by the other.


The agreements will last until one of the parties within wish to terminate this pact. A member of a hierarchy of the guild must contact opposing leader of this, and will no longer be obliged to follow this document. Any aggression shown to the opposing party will also result in the termination of this pact.




Final Words:
This pact is only valid between the two parties mentioned within, and neither are obliged to be at peace with parties they are allianced with.
