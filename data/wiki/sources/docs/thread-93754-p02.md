Guild Collaboration ​
Each guild within the Ordo Malleus alliance will be required to create a council of at least 4 members. This council must include :

- 1-2 War Commanders ( excused for merchant and community guilds)

- 1 Diplomat

- 1 Community Manager

- 1 Executive/Merchant (Optional)

Councils can be Either voted in by the members or chosen by the leader of the guild. Each member of the council will need to take part in different tasks:

1. War Commanders will apply themselves to lead, inspire the members of a guild and commit to defending and capturing areas the guild owns. They will also collaborate with other guilds War commanders to plan attacks on Enemy guilds and ensure the Alliance is protected at all times.

2. Diplomats are the voice of the guilds. Their task is to create, maintain and fix guild relations around the Alliance. Any problems that are inter-guild related must involve the diplomats to resolve it in a peaceful and calm manner and ensure the continuation of this great alliance.

3. Community Manager , One of the most important tasks of the council will fall to the Community managers, who will need to encourage member activeness. This can be done by events, drop parties, and other activities. Furthermore Community managers will aim to maintain the alliance reputation and entice the wider community to take part in Ordo Malleus Events.

4. Merchants/Executives are to ensure the LE keep flowing around the guilds and the alliances. They are to maintain the guild bank by adding more Emeralds to it, as well as making sure each member of their guilds has enough LE to acquire better gear and be in top shape.

The official Guild Councils will be Written up at a later date.
