Aequitas [Aeq]

Est. November 24th 2019

​
[image: [​IMG]]

The word Aequitas has a Latin origin, it means justice, equality, equity.

As the word, our guild is also meant to serve justice. Our main goal is to be righteous, equal. We don't tolerate disrespect, unrighteousness and if we experience it, we take action. We fight until the last breath, until justice is served.

[image: [​IMG]]

Aequitas has a unique rank system, based on a courtroom's personnel.

- Justices - members of the Supreme Court. They are the leaders of the guild. Overseeing it's function, and it's members.

- Chief Justice Frozenman [OWNER]

- Associate Justice Lalatera [CHIEF]

- Associate Justice xMini_ [CHIEF]

- Judges - trustworthy, handpicked members, who lead different parts of our guild. Some of them are part of the High Court, who help the Justices make larger decisions.

- Judge Drajenail [CHIEF]

- Judge Morticzek [CHIEF]

- Attorneys - trustworthy members, who have shown their suitability by participating in many guild wars. They have a basic idea of resource management, and are able to lead guild wars.

- Advocates - active members, who have at least one war class (one lvl95+ class with a proper build). Their task is to participate in guild wars, and are able to attack territories.

- Jury - active members of the guild, who proved their suitability by contributing to the guild in some way (xp contribution, activity).

- Witnesses - members of the guild.

A complete list of our members can be found here .
