A comprehensive guide to guilds, made by the only and real, Mocomochi.

Are you looking to get into the Wynncraft guilds but not so sure where to start? Whether you’re an old OG homie or a new player, this guide provides all the essential information about guilds, their benefits, how to join one, and where to find the perfect guild for you.

What Are Guilds?

Guilds are player-made communities that make players collaborate, compete, and socialize. They’re the only "stable" communities a player can find in-game that are run by the player base itself. Most of the time, it’s fun being in a guild, trust me.

Benefits of Joining a Guild

Being part of a guild has a few advantages, including:

- Community: A bunch of friends to talk to and play any activity with.

- Warring: One of the best endgames out there is only accessible through guilds, which is very hilarious, but you will surely enjoy it (not everyone does, though).

- Events: Many guilds host exclusive events, giveaways, or other activities.

- Guild Bank: A community-shared bank that can provide cool items for you and others in your guild.

- Guild Raids: The best and hardest raiding type in the game is only accessible through guilds. You gain more aspects/reward pulls by doing it.

- Tomes: The only way to obtain extra skill points is by joining a guild and getting a tome. Usually, most guilds give them for free if you just ask nicely, though some might have tome queues.

How to Join a Guild

Joining a guild is very easy, but here are some steps you might want to follow:

- Identify the Right Guild: Determine what you’re looking for in a guild (e.g., professions, warring, raiding, chatting, etc.).

- Check Requirements: Make sure you meet the guild’s level and other requirements.

- Submit an Application: Most guilds require an application, which can usually be found on their private Discord.

- Follow Up: Join their Discord server or reach out to a recruiter if needed.

Where to Find Recruiting Guilds

Finding the right guild is not hard. Use the following resources to find one:

- Recruitment Threads: The threads on the Wynncraft Discord are one of the best methods to find the guild you want. Just go there and look up the ones that match your level and preference.

- Party Finder: Some players open parties in /pfinder in-game to recruit new players. You might want to take a look at it.

- Shouts: Keep an eye on chat for guilds advertising over some big guilds.

Requirements for Joining a Guild

Each guild has its own requirements, but common ones include:

- Minimum Player Level: Some guilds require a specific level to make sure members can participate in wars or raids.

- Activity: Many guilds expect members to be active and contribute regularly.

- Discord Account: Talking to your community is usually a required thing, so you might want to have a Discord account.

Useful Links and Tools for Guild Members

Here are some resources to help you navigate the guild system:

- Live guild map: Link to the map

- Guild warring tutorial: Link to TheNelston's video

- Guild Recruiment Thread : Link to the thread

Guilds are, in my opinion, the most fun activity out there in Wynncraft. Seriously, I only spend my time doing guild-related stuff—might be a problem, idk. Anyways, thanks for reading this! If you need any help with guilds, check #guild_community in the Wynncraft Discord.
