Prime Minister Election Registration
Prime Minister:
The Prime Minister acts as a representative for the Gentry. He/She relays the wishes and requests of the Gentry to the Parliament, and vice versa. The Prime Minister can also plan events for the Gentry and does a variety of other jobs whenever the Parliament requests it.

Election Process:
A new Prime Minister will be elected every two months. The election process is as follows.
The election will last a week.
Three days will be dedicated to registration.
Three days will be dedicated to campaigning.
Voting will occur over the period of one day.

Election Requirements:
The only requirement for running is to be a part of the guild gentry.
