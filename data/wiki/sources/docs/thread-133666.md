The Separatists: ​

Not long ago, in a Minecraft server far far away (or not depending on where you live) a deadly enemy rose from the defeated corpse of Ordo Malleus. This enemy, known only has "Hack Forums" quickly established their rule through military might. Young aspiring guilds were crushed under the might of the tryhards, helpless to stop their advance. Soon after, another guild, Libertas, joined the surge. The two guilds crushed all opposition and still rule with an iron fist. Many distinguished scholars compare this rule of two to the ancient guilds of "The Nether's Ascent" and "Kangronomicon". But now, we have lived with their oppressive reign for far, far too long. It is time to take up arms against this phantom menace . Now, a new hope arises. The Separatists.

Any who oppose this rule, wish to battle, or want to be part of something just generally fun, join now. Regardless of why you fight, it is important that you do. The founding guild, The Anarchy, fights only to destroy the massive and oppressive guilds such as "Hack Forums" and "The Nether's Ascent". Join today, stop this madness!

Joining:

If you wish to join the fight, you can do so two ways.

One: You could join "The Anarchy", regardless of level or class.

Or Two: You could pledge your guild to The Separatists, and you way withdraw that allegiance whenever you feel the need. No guild is too small or too low level, heck, we are level 13! Strength in numbers!
