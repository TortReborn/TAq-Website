Here are the Tournament Rules:

Tournament Rules:

Main Rules:
- Breaking any of these rules may lead to an official warning or even a disqualification.
- For the outsiders who are watching the fight, do not buff the fighters with Strength/Resistance/Speed, and do not intrude the battleground.
- If you are not competing, remain in your respective area. (Atop the balcony in Llevigar Bank)

In-fight
- Everyone can obviously use their own build.
- Poison builds are banned upon request of the Federation leaders.
- Everyone has to use the same heal pots/ mana pots (Which is demanded lvl 75 heal pots/lvl 50 mana pots).
- No skillpoints pots like Strength/Defence, etc.
- No mythic Items allowed due the fact of being overpowered.
- Only tiers in armor/weapons from normal to legendary allowed.
- Don’t use any horses.
- Due the fact of lag/mishitting please do not use any disguises/pets. (Be aware some players may not have some super computer that can handle everything).
- Do not warp/tp/run outside the pvp area even though you are low. This is why you have health pots.
