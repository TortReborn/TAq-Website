Guild Summary:

PUN is a community and war guild.

[image: requirements.png]

- Level 60+ Combat Level on at least one class

- Like Corkus

- Be nice and courteous

- Agree to Rules

Discord:

We will send you an invite to our discord upon being accepted into the guild.

Discord is where we will make guild announcements, and have general chats.

Ranks:

Chief: Hand Picked.

Strategist: Experienced Captain

Captain: Are interested in wars, and active on Discord.

Recruiter: Participates in guild affairs (Ex. Talking in guild chat, doing wars)

Recruit: You've joined the guild. Congrats.

[image: Divider.png]

PUN has several different roles you can pick in order to be alerted of wars, grind parties, or to join the event team.

Pillagers:

- Actively gains xp for the guild.

- Recommended for those who have never been in a guild before

AHH (All Hands Hoay):

- This is the war team that we call upon when we need help protecting the homeland, and wars in general

- If you have never done a war before, this role may not be for you. See Pillager above.

Party Parrots:

- Our event team.

- Host events for the guild and community

-Spots are limited

- You must be recruiter+ to be able to join

- Additional application required (Message Quartermaster for application once recruiter+)
