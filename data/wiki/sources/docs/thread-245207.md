### post #1 — IceResistance (Titans Valor [ANO] Founder) — Mar 7, 2019 at 12:36 AM

The guild community is beautiful, but not without its flaws. We've to the same point in guilds that has been repeated countless times in history. You have so much dislike, so much tension in you for another guild revolving around war that you don't even see behind the territories, you don't even see the community, practically because there is none. Guilds are meant to bring players in Wynncraft together, but all I see is it keeping people apart. When you decided to join a guild, you instantly became part of a bigger picture; the guild community. But that name has been stained over the years not by the outside, but by us the players in which make it up. Every guild is vital towards the success of the guild community as a whole. When guilds aren't doing well, their internal communities lacking, the guild community as a whole lacks. The issue in which we currently face it the separation between war and the guild itself, pertaining to its community. Wars are suppose to be fun, a friendly competition over the map. Too often though do we end up taking that tension outside of war, and into the community itself, overall losing that separation. Then all of a sudden, war is community and then we end up with all these problems that promote discord between others because wars are no longer just for fun and is the center of our guilds as a whole. There needs to be a fine line that distinguishes relations from war from outside of war, otherwise contention continues to grow between guilds and players in which they house that divides everyone. If we don't fix this, everyone will end up hurt. And don't point fingers at other guilds, look at your own guild. If we keep blaming each other for the issues that are happening, then we won't ever reach a solid solution. The center of community has been poisoned by war, displaced and forgotten. No guild is better, or worse then an other, and every guild unique and diverse to another and you should be proud of that. We should all have the same goal in mind, of repairing the community. I'm not going to tell you how to fix it. That is up to you. You in your guild, you find your solution to how to combat this. The fate of the community is up to everyone. It is only us that can build it up.
