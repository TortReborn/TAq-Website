Raid Changes

- Replaced the Glutton's Gambit with the Famished's Gambit. Instead of only being able to take two buffs, you are now restricted to one less Raid Buff choice.

- Added the following Gambits:
- Fallen Angel's: After being airborne for 3 seconds, you are unable to regenerate health in any way for 5 seconds after landing.

- Fatalist's: Taking damage additionally deals 2% more damage for every second since last being hit. (Max 40%)

- Sadist's: You lose 2% of your current HP per second after 6 seconds of not dealing damage.

- Empath's: You receive 25% of the damage received from players 6 blocks around you.

- Reworked the following Gambits:
- Burdened Pacifist's:
- Old: For every 1 mob killed, reduce your buff durations by 10% or 4 seconds, whichever is greater.

- New: For every 2 mobs killed, reduce your received healing by 10% for 10 seconds.

- Leaden Fighter's:
- Old: For each 2% health you have, increase your spells mana cost by +1%.

- New: For every 1% mana you have above 40% of your max, increase your spells' mana costs by 1%.

- The Farsighted's and Myopic's Gambits now reduce damage by 90% instead of completely negating it.

- The Maddening Mage's Gambit now misfires your second spell instead of a random spell. The misfire requirement was increased from 10 to 15.

- Increased the enemy health multiplier in Guild Raids from +30% to +40%.

- Tweaked priority order of raid rewards to make it less likely for important rewards to get overwritten

-
The Nameless Anomaly (Guild Raid Boss) :
- Changed the Giant Void Hole attack:
- Now only triggers up to two times per fight, allowing the Nameless to teleport out of Void Holes onto players in quick succession.

- Now stops the Watched when triggered.

-
The Wartorn Palace (Intruding Rifts) :
- Rifts now glow a different color based on their challenge.

- The Hold Platform and Hold Rune rifts no longer use particles for their visuals.

- The same rift type no longer spawns twice in a row.

- More aggressive mobs targeting the guards now spawn more frequently.

- The raid objective now displays the amount of closed rifts.

- Guild Raid Change: Each rift now has an additional challenge to it.

- Added

- Reduced the damage boost Radiant Power grants to mobs.

Miscellaneous Changes

- Added Coop Mount Riding, allowing players to ride someone else's mount with them.

- The "Shared Tag"'s effects are more noticeable in the mid-game; items with the same elemental tag or skill points the Player has invested in will drop more frequently up to level 85.

- Quieted the Boss Altar activation sound.

Bug Fixes

- Fixed the Enraged Axon spamming its sound when hit repeatedly.

- Fixed Flight Burst removing Arrow Storm's recoil.

- Fixed Combo Stomp not working.
