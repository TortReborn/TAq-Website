### post #24 — Liliya (Well-Known Adventurer) — Nov 13, 2018 at 9:48 AM

It was a great time fighting you :P

For 6 months I have fought against The Federation and I didn't stop until it died.

It was very hard and I admit that we would have probably never gotten the power to overpower Fed if nobody had left it.

However, I met a lot of people and I am glad that we had this anti-Fed community.

But I wasn't happy with how split the Fed and anti-Fed communities were. There were occasions of threats and hate towards guilds or players mostly between Fed and anti-Fed guilds/players.

Now that Federation has fallen, I hope that we can come together as one community and mostly end this hate we had between each other. Some have tried it during Fed, for example Fox and BCr, but it didn't turn out as, at least I, expected.

Personally I joined Imperial as a community member when Federation fell and I haven't regretted it.

I plan on making the BuildCraftia discord fully public now as well.

To sum it up:

I'm glad that The Federation existed but it created issues community-wise and I think now is the perfect time to come together and actually get to know those who you have fought against for almost 9 months.
