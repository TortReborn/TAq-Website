Wardrobe Wonders (2.1.3)
The latest update has officially come in the form of Wardrobe Wonders. This update has introduced Lootrun Trials, a new in-game store with the Hero+ rank, reworks to cosmetics as well as even pets, a new account management dashboard for your profile, new aspects, and of course - a Trickster rework!
In-Game Store (2.1.3)
If you’ve had gold coins before they were unobtainable, you would no longer be able to access the gold coin shop (/gc), as that has now been retired. However, the new in-game store allows you to purchase more things with Silverbull shares, which now includes Ranks. You can also preview cosmetics as well, such as hats and other things, as well as a new use menu improvement. This is done by accessing the in-game shop.
Hero+
Essentially the middle ground between Hero and Champion, this rank is a budget option for those who want to have some Champion rank benefits, but don’t want to pay a large amount of money for such.
Pet Revamp
Most of the pets didn’t just get a remodel, they also got a revamp! Non-ranked players can now use pet abilities up to lvl 30, and how is that possible? The pet level cap (which was 20 for Hero ranks and below) has been removed. Now, you can have max level pets, regardless of your rank!

Account Management
Since the forums was the only account software used for, well, your Wynncraft account, there were a lot of issues and so forth, but lo and behold - Wynncraft accounts are here!

The mischievous and cunning archetype, useful for crowd control as well as being able to ‘trick’ the enemy has been overdue for a rework for a while, with the new addition including a new ability “The Malicious Mockery”, followed by “Noxious Haze”, as well as “Foul Play”
Fruma is ... Delayed?!
Unfortunately so. The Fruma expansion, or the Fruma Update, or 2.2 - has been delayed to early 2026 (I don’t have anything for a month in specific, but 2025 is no longer going to have Fruma), with an update being released on the 3rd of November 2025. To summarise, the physical size of Fruma would be rather small, but it would be rather dense, but this wouldn’t be the first time something like this has happened -
Salted has said that “ We have experience with a very similar situation 10 years ago. Gavel, our previous expansion, was not quite ready for the timeline we set, but we decided to get it out anyway. And although you always need to speed up a little bit near the end, we don’t want to overdo it and make the same mistake with something that important again. ”, which I wasn’t aware of what the heck Wynncraft was a decade ago, but that aside, 2026 is going to have a much more likely chance of having the Fruma expansion.

Brilliance
Sound the alarms and ready the circuits, Titans because Brilliance has done it again!After years of loyal service from our beloved ANO Bot (the original Valor), the team has pulled off one of the biggest technical overhauls in Titan history. The result? A brand-new, fully revamped, and now publicly available version of Valor!
The bot that’s powered Titans Valor for over four years has officially broken free from its home server and entered the world stage. Introducing the new Valor, the spiritual successor to our internal ANO Bot, rebuilt from the ground up and ready for everyone to use!
Valor Reforged
Originally planned as a light-weight spin-off called Valor Lite, the project quickly evolved into something much, much bigger. With C0rupted leading the charge on the massive slash command overhaul, Valor now boasts an entirely new command framework based around Discord’s official slash commands, along with cleaner backend logic, and smoother performance all around.
Much of the code has been entirely rewritten for optimization and commands are now neatly organized into groups for a more intuitive experience. For instance, /guild activity now replaces the old -activity, keeping everything tidy and consistent. And the best part? Anyone can now add Valor to their own server. Guilds across the community can make use of the same tools, now from the comfort of their own discord servers, whether it’s tracking wars, checking averages, or flexing that beautiful /map output.
Updates, Fixes, and Optimizations
Of course, Brilliance didn’t stop at a relaunch. The past few months have been packed with steady improvements and tweaks: - /warcount range input errors? Fixed!
- Guild icons now load faster (by like… 0.1 seconds, but we’ll take it).
- Guild Raids tracking has been expanded to include level 95+ guilds.
- /map can now handle territories with none claimed — no more empty maps of sadness!
- Backend optimizations ensure smoother deltas when players un-private their accounts, avoiding stat dumps on a single day.
And of course, with Wynncraft’s ongoing privacy adjustments, the Cabinet’s been quick to adapt and keep things consistent where possible. Expect a few rough edges as the API continues to change, but Brilliance is always on standby to patch things up!
What About ANO Bot and What’s Next for Brilliance?
Fear not, Titans! The old Valor (ANO Bot) isn’t disappearing, it’s simply staying behind the scenes to power our own internal guild functions. From here on out, all maintenance and updates will focus on the slash command Valor, with the old -commands being retired over time.
Between the public release and all the backend reworks, Brilliance has been busier than ever, but they’re still not done. Rumor has it that there are already new quality-of-life features in development and a few experimental commands being cooked up.
You didn’t hear it from us, but let’s just say Valor’s evolution has only just begun…
And that wraps up another monumental chapter in Brilliance history! From a small guild utility to a fully public powerhouse, Valor’s transformation marks a new era for Titans tech.

Main Stage: An Interview with Calluum
Written by Lamplesspost
Hi everyone! Welcome back to the main stage! In this edition I’m going to be asking our glorious leader Cal about his journey through ANO, as well as his thoughts on the current state of guilds in Wynncraft. I hope you enjoy and without further ado, let us begin!
What made you decide to join ANO?
I saw a thread and thought it looked like a cool guild.
How were guilds different when you first joined?
Everything was entirely different, guilds were entirely roleplay and prestige driven rather than materialistically like they are these days.
Have the changes been for better or worse?
I would definitely say for worse. Guild changes have forced smaller communities out and pushed an incredibly stale narrative of large guilds dominating the entire map, as well as letting toxic, bigoted guilds thrive.
How did you become the guild leader of ANO?
No one else wanted it when pally was stepping down, so I realised I'd need to step up and take on the mantle.
What’s the best thing about being a guild leader?
Being able to help maintain the guild in order to give others the opportunity I had.
What’s the hardest thing about being a guild leader?
Dealing with morons in and outside the guild.
What improvements do you think could be made to guilds?
I think there’s a lot that could be improved. Right now defending is high effort and low/no reward, so I think increasing the reward, making it less effort, or ideally both, would go a long way to improve guilds. Also I would reduce guild member caps and the maximum number of defendable territories to prevent megaclaims; reduce guild xp gain from guild raids to make other grinding methods more viable; and there’s always the obvious suggestion of simply adding more guild content to keep the guild scene fresh and interesting.
That’s all for the main stage this edition! Big thanks to Cal for letting me interview him, and a big thanks to you for reading. I’ll see you all next edition!
