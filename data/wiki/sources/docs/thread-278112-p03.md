That's what the secondary category is for. If you're a light war guild, your main purpose isn't warring so your primary wouldn't be set to warring, it would generally be set to community. Only guilds serious about warring should be labeled as warring as their primary.

On the note of subjectivity, I agree it's a bit of an issue, but numbers are numbers, they're easy to sort and it's something I can just look up easily. Trying to enforce something subjective would be a nightmare since it would require me to actively watch how guilds operate and make a decision, which the guild then may or may not agree with. It also isn't really important in the first place since the goal of this spreadsheet is to reduce the advantage of constantly bumping recruiting threads which helps smaller guilds who get less attention and applications by giving them a place to be seen and helps bigger guilds by putting them at the top of a list, which I think is a fair tradeoff. Ultimately the goal is for every guild to manage their own entry and while that's never going to happen, allowing guilds to promote themselves is the entire point and it's up to them on how they want to go about doing that.
