Introduction

At PROF, we strive to become to most tryhard profers in the game. Not a tryhard? No problem. You may join as a temporary member and leach off of us for prof bomb callouts, borrow our GXP gear/consumables, and more.

Our goal is to be able to provide territory GXP boost to Dernic professions to any player who needs it.

Temporary Members

We are still in direct relations with the prof discord server. (If you need an invite link to the prof server, DM me.) Anyone can join as a temporary member, no requirements are needed except to join the guild Discord. DM @ Crokee#0197 or any other Chief+ on Discord for the link. Temporary members have NO obligation to participate in anything guild related; all are voluntary. Permanent members CANNOT demand temporary members to do anything guild related, but are allowed to ask.

All temporary members are ranked as Recruit in-game.

DISCLAIMER!!! All temporary members are subject to being kicked out without notice to make space for other profers to abuse GXP territory boosts. If you want to get back in, simply message a Recruiter+ and they will let you back in.

.SpoilerTarget"> Spoiler: More info on the disclaimers for temporary members

- If you are new to Wynncraft or have not achieved endgame yet, this may not be the right guild for you. Although you may stay temporary despite this, you may find a lot of things we discuss are out of your level range. Yes, we have a great community of profers of all levels in conjunction with the prof Discord. However said, we highly do not recommend doing profs without completing your first playthrough.

- Some territory boosts may not be active. Ask a permanent member if you want to turn on a boost in a territory.

- Some territory boosts cannot be on due to guild circumstances such as territory loss, resources bugging out (1.20 bugs or backend updates haha), or simply not enough resource production to boost the requested territories.

- For long prof parties, we have a spreadsheet to guarantee maximum boost on all 4 territories, but it is unsustainable for longer than a weekend.

- You may be subject to getting kicked out without notice if the guild becomes full. We will kick out offline members who have been the most inactive first.

- You may stay for the prof community aspect if you would like, as long as there is space in the guild to do so. Please tell us that you wish to stay for a bit longer so that we don't accidentally purge you.

- NOTE: We may purge all temporary members after every DXP weekend unless you have stated that you want to stay after DXP weekend ends. Exceptions apply if you talk to us.

- We may temporarily promote you to recruiter/captain/strategist during times of crisis if you have shown to be helpful in holding our claims.

We can hold a maximum of 86 players, which is enough for a full server + 41 permanent members. As a temporary member, you are free to move in and out of the guild to go to your main guild if you need to, no questions will be asked.

To be a permanent member, continue reading below.

Community Members

Community members function similarly as temporary members, but they are deemed as permanent members. These members will never be kicked without notice, unless through demotion.

These members are ranked as Recruiter.

War Members

War members are permanent members who war for PROF. They are tasked to defending SE to keep GXP territory boosts going.

These members are ranked as Captain and higher.

Applying for Community Member

If you want to be a community member DM @ Crokee#0197 or any other Chief member on Discord with the following information:

- IGN?

- Discord Username?

- What are your prof levels? Combat levels?

- Main reason for joining the guild?

- How will you help the community aspect of the guild?

- Any other information you would like to tell us?

In addition, join the guild as a temporary member for a few days before applying.

You may reply on this forum thread, but please note that we rarely check the forums. Please answer all 6 questions in your message when you DM us.

Applying for War Member

If you want to be a war member you must fill out this form here. Do not reply to this forum thread nor DM us if doing so. You may input any response for questions relating to profs, though having an actual answer to those questions may help you have a better chance of being accepted. For the last question, please include your war experiences and anything else relating to warring that you know of. We may DM you for further questions if needed.

Prerequisites to qualify for applying:

- At least 1 lv 103+ class that has unlocked the entirety of SE.

- Have a war build ready that's NOT KoH healer.

- Bonus points if you have a Gathering profession at Lv 110+.

- Must already be in the guild as a temporary member for at least a few days.

- Be involved in the prof community.

- Either in prof discord or in the prof guild as a temporary member.

- Must agree to not have ANY affiliation with any prior major guild that held several territories over long periods of time. Meaning to resolve any potential conflicts of interest relating to other guilds.

Requirements as a war member:

- Know the prof community well.

- Be willing to war to take back any lost territories in SE. You may NOT war to take any territory outside of SE, except for FFAs.

- Keep the guild as neutral as possible towards all other guilds. No bias towards any guild.

- Follow 7's spreadsheet on defenses for territories and scenarios that come up.

- Allocate wood resources to requested areas from profers for GXP boost if strategist+.

- You may not leave and join the guild to be involved in other major guilds.

- Be able to communicate with other guilds while keeping an unbiased stance towards them.

Borrowing Church

Need to borrow church to level up your own guild? DM @ Crokee#0197 or any other Chief+ on Discord for further information.
