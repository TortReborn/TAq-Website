# Thundderr (KingdomPhoenixes and Holders of LE) — oral testimony

Recollections from a member of KingdomPhoenixes during its first season, relayed
in 2026.

## Standing

He was one of the guild's two founding chiefs, styled "Prince of the Phoenixes",
and AurumKitsune passed him the crown on 22 November 2017 — eighteen days after
the events described here. He is therefore a witness to them and not a compiler
of them, which is worth more than most of the testimony this wiki carries. It is
still memory, offered nine years afterwards, by someone inside the guild that
did the removing.

## The account

On the November 2017 break in KingdomPhoenixes:

> Whimsy and Aurum had a big disagreement, which resulted in Aurum kicking her.
> Whimsy and some others then started the Serpentem Empire.

## What it settles

The guild's two internal documents had been recorded in the wiki as
disagreeing about this event: the dated event log has three officers removed
for "high treason", while the history table has members leaving of their own
accord and founding the Serpentem Empire. That was read as a contradiction.

This testimony makes it a sequence — a quarrel, then a removal, then a founding
— under which both documents are describing true and consecutive things. It
does not settle the one-day difference between them (3 versus 4 November), and
nothing here corroborates the event log's charge of "high treason", which
remains that document's characterisation rather than an established fact.

The founding it describes is independently attested: the Serpentem Empire [SNK]
appears in the Coalition's roster at the declaration of war on 21 December 2017
— seven weeks after the split — in Drew1011's account, and the War Syndicate
charter names ElegantDeath, one of the three officers the event log says were
removed, as its leader.

## How the wiki treats it

Claims resting on this source alone are attributed in the prose. No transcript
is published: the material used from it is in the articles that cite it.
