### post #64 — aNameless (Nezaract Head Division) — Nov 25, 2018 at 3:54 PM

Though I am very late, I would like to give one last thanks. Better late than never, right?

I was never apart of The Federation, as most people probably were, I was usually on the more "neutral with whoever" side, but I have large respect for the alliance, and I have equal respect for those who fought against Fed as well. Though each side had their differences, those sides being Federation and Anti-Fed, along with us neutrals of course, it was a fun time. Yes, some situations weren't the greatest, and did affect the community a bit, but it still had some sort of purpose and meaning to it. With the fall of former greatness, comes the opportunity to start again. Let's make this new era a good one! Long Live Federation, and Long Live the bond we have all formed with one another. Let's see this as an opportunity to reconnect on a more friendly terms, and see past our differences.
