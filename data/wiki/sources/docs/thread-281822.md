1.20: Gavel Reborn is OUT ! Play it now at play.wynncraft.com

The update brings a lot of new stuff:

- Housing

- Raids

- A lot of new and reworked quests

- A lot of new discoveries

- Guild Reworks

- New Forgery

- New CHAMPION rank and store perks
