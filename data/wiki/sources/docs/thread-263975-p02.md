### post #25 — Burnette — Mar 9, 2020 at 8:53 AM

Pretty offtopic but I feel like I should say something.

As a person who's come from bigger guilds to start a new one, it always is hard to get on the map and stay there. That being said, it's up to you if you want to keep up the effort. You don't always have to be on the map; please dear god, I'm begging you, do not overwork yourself and stay up for hours and hours warring. Wynn is supposed to be fun, and I hate it when I see people get burnt out and quit.

I'm in the guild oxo, and the war group is usually 2-3 of us warring (well tbh my war commander hard carries my guild lmao). Not many people are interested in warring, so our little group gets a small bit bored after a few hours. When you're in a dry rut, make a guild event to spend time with your members! Do LI, a scavenger hunt, or hell, I even schedule hide and seek events. Or play games outside minecraft together, like scribblio or cards against humanity. It's also a fantastic way to get to know your members and build a bond between you all.

That being said, to everyone still reading this, I wish you all good luck on your guilds, regardless of whether they're new, old, or you're planning on making a guild. ♡
