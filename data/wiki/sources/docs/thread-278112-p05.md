### post #84 — Ingo (Class Building Enthusiast) — Feb 10, 2021 at 2:36 PM

Recruiting - Is the guild looking for new members? Open guilds are taking applications from anyone who meets their requirements, If a guild becomes inactive or is no longer looking to recruit new members, it will be marked as Closed. Invitation means the guild will ask you to join them. If you are an owner and have editing permission, please use this when you are no longer taking applications.

- Open

Guild Code and Name - The guilds 3 (or 4) letter code and name. You probably want to know the name of the guild you're applying for and it should make looking for more info on them using Wynncraft's website easier.

- WCu

- The Warrior Cult

Language - The main language spoken by the guild. If you're a guild who are mostly one language but allow speakers of another language to join, you may want to include that in the additional notes section and if you only allow speakers of one language other then English that must be included in the additional requirements section.

- English

Guild Level - Level of the guild. This is included to determine the listing order since it's more fair then alphabetical. If you want a smaller or newer guild your best bet is to look from the bottom up. Guild must reach level 30 in order to be added to this list.

- 36

Guild Size and Creation- How many people (on average) are in the guild and when the guild was made. Small is <20, Medium is 21-40 and large is 40+ members. If a guild is on the fence between two groups, it is marked as the smaller category.

- Small

- Made at the end of the summer holidays iirc but the community discord already existed some time before that

Type and Secondary - The primary (and secondary) purpose of a guild. Warring means lots of wars, Community means events outside of wars are planned and hosted, Trading will probably become more relevant in 1.20, but for now it's for guilds who lootrun and run a guild shop, Roleplay is for guilds who roleplay, Event/Game is for guilds who are more of a metagame then a guild, and Meme is for, well meme guilds and Other is for if you do something very specific which falls outside the scope of the previous 7. If you are a "other" guild, please say why in the additional information section.

- Community

- Event/Game

Required Level - Most guilds require you to be a minimum level to apply, this is that level. Any means they're open to applications from anyone, however most guilds of that nature expect to see you increase your level over time, so keep that in mind when applying.

- You have to join our discord and in order to get chat access there you need a lvl 21+ warrior, exception if warrior is your only class, then it can be lower

Required Activity - Minimum amount of playtime per week/month and required guild contributions in order to maintain a spot in the guild. (Planned to add)

- Nothing atm, help in wars is appreciated but not needed

Additional Requirements - Some guilds require applicants to have met other requirements beyond level like having a Discord (very common), being above a certain age or having a minimum number of hours. This is where those requirements are listed. This section may also contain information about what the guild expects of you, such as xp or emerald contributions.

- Discord (dm me (Ingo#5621) or any other warrior cult discord member)

Additional Notes - This is for guilds to fill in themselves, can be a short message they want to tell people, can be a pitch, can be anything really. Most guilds won't have something here unless they asked for permission to edit the page.

- You have to enjoy playing warrior

Special Ranks - Some guilds use more ranks then the default four in the game like supreme emperor or strategist to name a couple. If that's the case, they'll be marked as yes. These guilds tend to have more room for promotion and specialization so if you're interested in that this should help you out.

- No, promotion depends on your activity, time in the guild and if you are trustworthy

Alliances - Some guilds are in alliances, this is where they're listed. This should help you see what guilds are connected so you don't accidentally end up in a rival faction to a friend.

- None

Thread and Wynn Links - Thread links to the page where you can find more details and apply to the guild and Wynn links to the guilds stats page on Wynncraft.com.

- https://forums.wynncraft.com/threads/the-warrior-cult-wcu.275811/#post-3300907
