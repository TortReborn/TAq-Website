Who are we looking for? We're looking for players from around the world who have at least one high-level class, an average play time of at least a few hours near daily, and we do require you to have and use Discord. It's preferable that you can actually use your microphone on Discord, but it's not completely required you do. It's heavily preferable if you enjoy guild wars, or if you have a strong understanding of Wynncraft and its mechanics. We're also looking for people with strong leadership skills and those willing to take initiative. Please note we have an age requirement of 15+ for both In-game and in discord to help better moderate our community.
