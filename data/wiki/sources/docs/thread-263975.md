If an alliance owns the map I don't think we should regulate how the territories are divided I think that's stupid, whoever's in the position of power should make the territory distribution rules, but that's a given. Final word: At some point every guild on the map was just a small guild who ended up working their way up to a position of power, let's not pretend every guild on there was a powerhouse from day one except for guilds that were created by already experienced and skilled warrers like Oys which is the first one that came to mind.
