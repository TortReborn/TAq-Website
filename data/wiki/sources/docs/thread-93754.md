The Order of the Hammer

The Ordo Malleus Alliance is a collection of guilds around the provinces of Wynn and Gavel who have united to have fun, claim territories and enjoy themselves. We are a dynamic alliance of War, Community and Trading guilds, supporting each other to have fun experiences, be the best, and provide the server with fun events in which everyone can take part in. Here, we promote a sense of family and community, and encourage each guild within the alliance to treat their allies and the greater community as equals and with respect.

Guild List

Guild Count: 10 (+1 unofficial organisation)

DeathReapers [ DxR ] (War Guild) - led by jjaayyddoonn

The guild of Death Reapers is a nice and friendly guild, aiming to achieve ever higher and become the best. With strong players, and supporting leadership the guild has made great leaps to where they are now.

Wynn Theory [ WTh ] (War Guild) - led by PaladinArenos

Wynn Theory was founded on the principles of competition and integrity, and its main goal is before anything else, is to have fun. Wynn Theory is all about striving to improve and get better all the time; meaning we fully intend to compete for the top spot. It is always looking for people that can help WTh's community with quest, leveling, most importantly, guild wars.

Holders Of LE [ HoL ] (War Guild) - led by Drew1011

Holders of LE is a guild of players which always strive to be the best. With their helpful members and strong will, they have overcome many obstacles (including many changes in leadership) to achieve their goals while having fun and enjoying bonding with one another in the guild. They are a family brought together by the spirit of war and community.

Kangronomicon [ Fuq ] (War Guild) - led by Dar_krusos

Strong and unmoving, the guild of Kangronomicon has been one of the greatest and most influential powers within Wynn. With radiant players and a strong, well-maintained leadership, Kangro is a force with which to be reckoned.

Libertas [ Lir ] (War Guild) - led by yosteric5

The Libertas guild, being both friendly and active, has always portrayed a strong trust towards players. Members of this guild are loyal, lively, and enjoy fighting battles and capturing territories from all around Wynn.

Necronomicon [ Feq ] (War Guild) - led by LordMuttonChops

The Necronomicon guild , is one of the oldest allies of Fuq and is a guild where everyone can feel wanted. Described as the "kids" of Ordo Malleus, you can expect to find a lot of fun here, all without being too immature, and still keeping respect to others.

White Wolves [ WWA ] (Semi - Community) - led by Grimmcat

The White Wolves are a small fighting force of mighty players who strive to help the people of Wynn in any way they can. The members within this guild enjoy having fun and supporting the whole Wynn community as well as their own friendly and lighthearted group.

Wynn Wide Merchants Organisation [ WWMO ] (Merchant Organisation) - led by The Three Members of Four

While there are few of these talented merchants around Wynn, each own a considerable amount of rare item collectibles and Liquid Emeralds. This only merchant organisation prides itself on fair trading and sponsorship of various events hosted by guilds across the province of Wynn. Led by the mysterious Three Members of Four the organisations true goals remain to be seen.

Guild Application

Ordo Malleus is an expanding family of guilds that above all else, respects, supports and maintains good relationships with one another, allowing each other to grow stronger. With our new application systems, new starting and evolving guilds can begin to join in our ranks and help with territory wars and community events. We accept all guilds who meet the requirements and can allow a place within our alliance for active guilds.

Alliance Rules

1. Have fun and respect one another; your fellow guild allies and every member outside of the alliance.

2. No hacking or cheating.

3. Refrain from attacking allies’ territories.

4. All inter-guild problems must be resolved calmly and as soon as possible.

5. Do not insult or criticize other guilds and members.

The Passage of Kinship

Alone.. It has been said that those of us within the guilds make others suffer, separated from the glory of Wynn's community...

But none of us, are truly ever alone.

For our guild hearts are bound by honor, tradition....

Our battles are waged in the name of the many. The courageous who day after day choose the Alliance of Ordo Malleus.

- PaladinArenos

The Blacksmiths of Peace

Here we are, the Blacksmiths of Peace,

Who carry out the Will within the Law.

We parade in capes of gold fleece

Routing those who keep men in thrall

As we keep the world from staining cerise.

With blood and grit we rule upon the beast;

The beast of lands which upon it we feast

With many a-eyes, we look to the east.

It is there we shall find the vibrant sun

As vibrant as we, our pride bright, ne’er ceased.

United! Exult! We as one cause fear;

Power’n glory, with honor not flammer.

For we alone are not standing bare here

Our shields together, under The Hammer

United en masse ‘til the final year.

Our beginning come in a leap;

Evil’s end exposed; life runs thin.

It will soon bare only to weep,

‘Tis they who keep committing sin

From which we, Our Justice, shall come to reap.

- Dar_krusos
