The History of Titans Valor
	

Below is an archive of the history of the Titans Valor guild. While most historical events are documented, some past events are still being recovered and added to our archive. This timeline was established as a resource to guild members to reflect on our guild’s history, and to see how far we have come as a guild.


Date                Serving                   Affiliated                    History
                        Emperor                 Alliance
	

July, 2017
	Emperor Ice
	The Emperium of Wynn 
	* Titans Valor was established July 2nd, 2017 as a community guild by IceResistance, the guild’s founder
* The guild would be extended an invitation to The Emperium of Wynn [TEoW] alliance. This invitation would lead to Titans Valor becoming more part of the guild community.


	August, 2017
	

	

	* Titans Valor would officially enter the Emperium of Wynn alliance early August, mostly an alliance for community guilds. 
* Guild officials would achieve Emperium positions later, or upon joining. 
* The guild would remain a community guild, until far later on.
	

	

	

	

	October, 2017






















	

	War Syndicate Alliance
	* Titans Valor would be voted out of the Emperium of Wynn alliance on October 11th. A few guilds would leave the alliance as a result of this leading to the alliance falling.
* The guild was shortly invited to the War Syndicate alliance, an alliance that would seize power from HackForums after their reign had lasted over a year.
* The guild thread is recreated on the 14th.
* War Syndicate would give off the province of Wynn, as planned, to newer guilds to grow. The Coalition alliance would form from this opportunity on October 18th.
* Titans Valor would seize all island territories, and would hold them for a long period of time.
* The Titans Valor Shop is established on the 30th.








	November,
2017














	

	

	* After a while, the guild would adopt an imperial hierarchy, the guild now becoming the empire of Titans Valor.
* Tensions between Coalition and War Syndicate grew during this time period, resulting in December being a month of war, and celebration for the guild.
	December,
2017
	

	

	* The Titans Valor Winter Festival thread is launched on December 1st. All members of the Wynncraft community are invited.
* The guild would celebrate their achievement in becoming level 51 on December 15th, one day before the Winter Festival took place.
* As tension grew, the Coalition alliance would declare war on December 21st, which would initiate one of the largest wars in history, the Gavellian Invasion.
* December 23rd, three guilds left War Syndicate, leaving the others to abandon the defence of Gavel.
* In an attempt to recover, Titans Valor forms the Aphelion alliance, which would later fall to ruins when Coalition plans change and the land gained by the alliance is infiltrated. 
* Coalition forces would obtain all territories on the map between their allied guilds, and surrendered foreigners on the 25th.
	

	

	

	

	January, 2018
	

	

	* Titans Valor would be left without an alliance, battling the Coalition. 
* The remnants of War Syndicate bind together to do damage to Coalition territories, although it would not be considered an official alliance yet, although we were under the name of Illustratus.
* This month would show Titans Valor the downs every guild would eventually go through.
	February, 2018
	

	The Federation
	* Titans Valor would adopt a medal system to award achievements to its members, consisting of 8 medals.
* Titans Valor would lead many successful attempts to wipe out guilds from the Coalition, although the territories would be back in Coalition possession shortly after.
* On February 16th, HackForums, Paladins United, Emorians, Kangronomicon and House of Sentinels would leave the Coalition alliance, resulting in the remaining half of the Coalition combining with the old remains of War Syndicate, Illustratus. The alliance formed was the Federation.
* On the same day of the split of the Coalition, the guild would establish a sub-guild named Illustratus [Ius].
* On February 19th, the guilds that had originally left Coalition were officially wiped out by the Federation after claiming all the map.
	March, 2018
	

	

	* IceResistance appoints Dark5010 as serving emperor of Illustratus.
* On March 24th, the first Titans Valor Award Ceremony would take place.
