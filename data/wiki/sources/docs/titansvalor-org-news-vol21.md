Main Stage: A Dive into Fruma
Written by DragonZftw & l1npengtul
After years of teasers, Wynncraft 2.2: The Fruma Expansion finally released on the 4th of April, 2026. Following a 13 part questline following a formal royal summon from Her Majesty, the clandestine Monarch of the Fruma province, the Royal Gates are finally opened, and the player is finally let back inside, to where it all began.
Warning: The following sections will spoil the entirety of the Fruma questline. The quests, cutscenes, and writing are quite enjoyable in this update so we highly recommend going in blind.
The entire questlines requires that the player have already completed the main questline quests in other regions, specifically:
- Recover the Past (Lv. 25)
- The Realm of Light (Lv. 79 - final part of the The Realm of Light questline)
- A Journey Further (Lv. 101 - final part of the Silent Expanse questline)
Chapter I: Homecoming
- Queen’s Recruit (Lv. 103): The Royal Selection officially begins! Her Majesty invites the Player, Tasim, and many other characters you have met along your journey to Fruma. There can only be one winner, but the tournament quickly goes sideways.
- A Journey Home (Lv. 104): The player left agasp in front of the sealed Royal Gate… wondering if that was that. By going back to where the player came and with the help of the Caravan driver, the player discovers that curiosity kills the cat.
Chapter II: Leaves in the Wind
- A New Beginning (Lv. 105): After the events of the previous quest, Espren is off-limits to those without identification papers; including the player. While attempting to obtain and destroy a military report, the player finds themselves meeting the Leaves in the Wind.
- The Cursed One (Lv. 106): We meet Her Majesty again in the Auburn Festival, and as preparations are underway for the festival, Syndra, the leader of The Leaves in the Wind, requests that you teach the others about Wynn and its magic. During the festival, Syndra can be spotted talking to an… auspicious figure.
- Revelations in Fall (Lv. 108): The Crown begins to close in on The Leaves in the Wind, and as it is no longer safe the player and the group decide to move into the Roughworks, acquiring passports from an unexpected individual.
Chapter III: The Steel Feather
- Solidary of Steel (Lv. 109): After staying low in the Roughworks, The Leaves in the Wind regroup in Timasca, and we are introduced to a new group headed by a familiar face, the Steel Feather, who are based in an abandoned train station.
- The Price of Ingenuity (Lv. 110): Something is off about one of the brothers, Yusanu. He disappears into his workshop for hours on end, seemingly hard at work; however no sound can be heard from outside the workshop. Rex and the player decide to investigate and end up making a perhaps impulsive and rash decision.
- Echoes of Change (Lv. 112 - The Missing Piece (Lv. 111) completion recommended ): The festival for the Roughworks approaches near, and Her Majesty is expected to make an appearance. Tired of always being on the run away from the Crown, the resistance decides to take a stand, despite forewarning by a familiar face. The Resistance’s success, however, is all but certain.
Chapter IV: Amongst the Canopy
- Shrouded in Mist (Lv. 113): The fight at the festival ended in tragedy, and due to a request by Syndra to keep them safe, the player takes Sui and Yusanu to the Mistwoods, with help from an unlikely defector. Finding refuge in a cave, the group suddenly finds themselves at spear-point, but ends up joining another resistance cell, the Canopy.
- True Colours (Lv. 115): Changlings, the dangerous shape-shifting spies that the Crown uses to gain intelligence on activities happening within the Mistwoods. The Canopy catches one of these spies in a cell, and using special interrogation methods find the whereabouts of the other members of the Leaves in the Wind and Steel Feather. Yunasu, Sui, and the player are directed to travel to the Owl headquarters, but a certain Changling decides a change of plans. Will Sui and the player make it out of the Sovereign's grasp?
Chapter V: The Blossom of Blades
- Overture to Despair (Lv. 116): Sui and the player move from the Mistwoods to Hyloch in order to find the surviving Leaves in the Wind members, who are currently imprisoned at the Highland Sovereign's dungeon. Along the way, Sui and the player encounter a very particular resistance group, The Blossom of Blades, and the player’s silence leads to the end of a long friendship.
- Ensemble of Hope (Lv. 118 - Off the Rails (Lv. 109) and The Strong Survive (Lv. 117) completion recommended): Rex, Sui, Zeph, and the player regroup in Hyloch, and with the assistance of the Canopy and the surviving Steel Feathers members, a new plan to take down the Crown is formulated. Sui and the player, along with the help from a disgruntled Bleeding Rose, an ensemble of the Leaves in the Wind, Steel Feathers, Canopy, and Blossom of the Blades form an assault on the looming capital, Aelumia Citadel.
- Apotheosis (Lv. 119): The final part of the Fruma questline, the player is notified that Rex, Sui, Yunasu, and Captain Cileon have encountered trouble at Hyloch, leading to a fight with the Highland Sovereign, Majin. Her Majesty, tired of the rebels, decides to destroy the Royal Dam, which would flood the Auburn Forest. Defeating both Her Majesty and the final Sovereign, Rex is killed but ultimately revived, and the player is tasked with closing the rifts that now are visible above Fruma by entering The Wartorn Palace. - After the end of Apotheosis, Tasim can be spotted behind the Leaves in the Wind hideout, near a grave. A letter from Aledar can be read, thrown away in a nearby pot.
- Make sure to return to Fort Torann to open the Royal Gate in order to be able to access the Province of Fruma and The Wartorn Palace Raid on any class.

Side Quests
- Undersupply (Lv. 107, Auburn Forest): The festival has caused the water allocated to the people of the Auburn Forest to be reduced even further, and the player is tasked with entering the Royal Dam to open the dam so the villagers may drink.
- The Scarred Springs (Lv. 108, Auburn Forest): In a quiet corner of the Auburn Forest, the Pishlas Hot Springs sit dilapidated, its waters dirty. Accompany Maripe in restoring the Pishlas Hot Springs to what it once was, and help in getting the word out to the Auburn folk.
- Off the Rails (Lv. 109, Roughworks): Upon boarding one of the trains in the Fruman Underground by finding a misplaced ticket, a murder mystery unfolds as the General of the Provincial Guard is found murdered in her cabin. The player is tasked with collecting evidence to make an accusation to find the murderer. - Correct or otherwise, you will receive all the rewards upon completing the quest.
- This quest unlocks the Fruman Underground, a very powerful method of fast travel between the Fruma regions.
- An animation plays every time you ride the Fruman Underground, and the animation that the player model does every time is different.

- Through the Pipes (Lv. 110, Roughworks): Help Fichu find their package, which was stolen by a racoon by going through the Roughworks sewer system. - The final reward differs based on the player’s performance on the last section of the quest.

- The Missing Piece (Lv. 111, Roughworks): An overworked Postmaster asks the player to bring a stack of letters to an elderly resident, Elise, who lives nearby. Reading the letters, Elise decides to hold a small picnic on a nearby hill, and remembers. - This quest is very buggy. Do not leave the quest during the factory section - otherwise you risk softlocking the quest. There is currently no way to unsoftlock the quest.
- This quest is important for Syndra’s background.

- Burning Bonds (Lv. 114, Mistwoods): Abramod lost his medallion, and asks the player to help them make another so they may re-enter Aldwell. - This quest is required for a secret discovery.
- This quest unlocks Adasaurs, a dinosaur-like mount.

- Celebrations in Smoke (Lv. 115, Mistwoods): The Mistwoods Festival is underway, however is interrupted when someone attempts to commit arson. The leader of Aldwell asks the player to investigate 3 people.
- The Strong Survive (Lv. 117, Highlands): Arnol, a child of noble blood, finds himself sent to a military academy after failing to get into Aelumia University. The academy is more corrupt than it seems however... - Arnol remakes his appearance in the final 2 quests of the main storyline, Ensemble of Hope and Apotheosis . If you complete this quest, he can be seen at the Canopy Hideout (Ensemble of Hope) and then at Aelumia Citadel (Apotheosis) as a resistance member.
