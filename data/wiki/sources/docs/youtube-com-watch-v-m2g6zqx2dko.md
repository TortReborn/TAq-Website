I believe that Wynncraft's guilds are in a bad state right now, and here's why.

Hello everyone, this is TheNelston, and welcome back to another video. Today, I’m going to be talking about one of the most underappreciated systems in Wynncraft, guilds. This issue is really near and dear to my heart, as the current owner of Idiot Co, which is one of the larger guilds on the server. While saying that guilds are bad is a take as cold as the other side of my pillow until the Rothschilds pay the pillow company to magically make both sides almost as hot as me, I wanted to dissect the issues more thoroughly. In my view, it makes no sense, after all, that guilds in an MMORPG shouldn’t take center stage when it comes to group activities. To that end, I will be discussing several broad issues: the messy state of guild wars, the lack of content for lower level players and non-warrers in general, and the absence of many quality of life features. It is important to note, however, that I will be explicitly avoiding discussing solutions to these problems, as that would take so long as to require an entirely separate video to cover and I am not a developer, at any rate. Ultimately, I want to answer one simple question: why should we care about guilds in Wynncraft? Let’s get right into it.
