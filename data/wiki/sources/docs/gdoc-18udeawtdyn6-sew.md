Leaving Ordo Malleus:


Hello. So after HHU and TnP left, this alliance quickly became something it never should be. In my opinion, instead of this alliance being a balance between all the guilds, it become more of a dictatorship between Kru and Arenos. HHU and TnP were two of HoL's best friends. It is a known fact that the alliance was largely ran by Fuq, HHU, HoL, Tnp, and Snr. These were the original guilds, and were the most respected Ordo guilds. There was an issue that these guilds were split on. You can probably guess what it is. When HHU and TnP left, HoL was the only one left on one side of the issue. It is in my opinion that this caused the issue to grow, and I was powerless against it, with nobody on my side. I have begun to see Ordo for what it is, a group of people who judge everyone else, and look down upon every other person, and believe that this alliance is no longer beneficial to HoL. I regret to inform the leaders of Ordo Malleus that HoL will be leaving the alliance to pursue other interests. This alliance has been a great experience, and I wish some of you the best in your future endeavors. I don't want you to think I am taking this decision lightly. I have been sitting here for about 10 minutes trying to make myself hit the send button. To the people here I enjoy talking to and spending time with (you know who you are), I'm sorry.
