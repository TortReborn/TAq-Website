A lot of you probably saw this in the Wynn discord but this is for any of you who aren’t in that discord. Here’s the announcement Salted made just now:
“Hey @everyone

The unofficial, player-made modpack "Wynncraft Enhanced" was noticed by our administration to be potentially harmful.
The pack is configured to relay your data through a third party proxy.
This means that any Minecraft data sent or received by your computer while the mod was active (even in the background) may have been compromised.
We advise everyone who may have used this mod at one point to change their Minecraft password and email associated with it just in case.

The modpack is hosted on the Curseforge website and is not in any way affiliated with Wynncraft.
Using Minecraft client mods always come with risks as they allow developers to change your client and affect your computer in many different and sometimes unexpected ways.
Please be careful when using any of them while playing Minecraft.
We typically only discourage their use but not outright ban them, however, for this specific case we will shortly be restricting joining the server with the mod enabled.

Wynncraft was designed to work completely mod-free and we still recommend this way of playing for the safest experience.

- The Wynncraft Staff”
