1.18 , also known as the "Economy Update" was a major update to Wynncraft released on January 18th 2019. It was first announced in a trailer video .

Additions

Professions

- Includes Gathering and Crafting professions
- 4 Gathering professions
- Woodcutting

- Mining

- Fishing

- Farming

- 8 Crafting professions
- Weaponsmithing

- Woodworking

- Armouring

- Tailoring

- Jeweling

- Alchemism

- Cooking

- Scribing

- Each profession is leveled individually, with each having a level cap. of 132

- Professions allow players to craft and create custom weapons, armor, accessories and consumables

- In order to craft an item, players must first gather materials from Gathering nodes with Gathering tools

- Materials come in different types and qualities, with better material types being unlocked at higher Gathering levels

- Added Ingredients
- Up to 6 Ingredients can be used in a crafting recipe to add Identifications to the crafted item and modify its durability/duration and requirements

- Ingredients are mostly got from mob drops and loot chests

- Added Refineries and Tool Merchants around the map

Mini-Quests

- Added Slaying post Mini-Quests

- Short 1-stage Quests without characters or a story, in which the player must bring a certain amount of Ingredients to the Slaying post

- The Ingredients are acquired from mobs near the Slaying post

- Rewards include Combat XP

General

- Added one new Corrupted Dungeon, Corrupted Ice Barrows

- Added the Trade Market

- Redesigned Sand-Swept Tomb and Corrupted Sand-Swept Tomb to have a new mechanic of random rooms

- Added Secret Discoveries for the Desert region

- Hovering over a player's name shows their past usernames

- Extended the explorable Void region under the Sky Islands

- Increased the maximum level from 101 to 104

- Added jump pads to Llevigar

- Added elevators to Molten Heights and Ahmsord

- Added many new mob models

Changes

- Reworked a very large amount of builds

- Item Buyers have been replaced by Blacksmiths , who can also scrap items and repair crafted items in addition to buying items

- Improved the XP requirement curve in mid-game

- Changed Dungeon token collectors from hoppers to clickable texts

- Changed Dungeon minimum level requirement to be -5 of the Dungeon's level instead of -15

- Doubled Bank storage size

- Buffed all Ring Accessories
