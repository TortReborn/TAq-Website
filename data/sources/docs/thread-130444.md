On June 5th, 2016, the Halcyon Alliance was born.

The Halcyon Alliance was founded upon the vision to create solutions of preparing for success, improving community ideals and player relations.

This is an agreement for whenever help is needed, each member shall aid each other in whatever task it may be. Each guild shall work together

to discuss any issues that may arise and compromise to reach agreement. We hope to organize events and challenges for a way for members to

show off their skills and greatest triumphs, and essentially converse well with others.

​

[image: [​IMG]]

​
The alliance was initially formed between the two guilds, ToastedPandas and Holders of LE, in order to create a sense of unity between the two.

[image: [​IMG]]

[image: [​IMG]]

​

[image: [​IMG]]

This alliance will serve to connect the guilds together, while maintaining respect for each guild as a whole as well as guild’s people.

The members of each guild are expected to treat each other with dignity. Internal affairs are to be handled by individual guild leaders, while

matters concerning foreign entities as well as within the whole alliance are to be dealt with by every guild leader and any other representatives.

Our number one goal is to provide an environment where members can feel comfortable socializing and gaming.
