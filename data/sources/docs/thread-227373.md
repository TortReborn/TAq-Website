Hi Wynn Forums! I am Simply the leader of Sins of Seedia, presenting you with an alliance and bond between 4 guilds, also known as The Aesir Pact, or even simpler, Aesir.

What is Aesir?

Aesir is an alliance, or if you will sister-ship pact, between 4 warring and growing community guilds:

Fantasy [Fux]

Sins of Seedia [SDU]

IceBlue Team [IBT]

DiamondDeities [DDT]

We are committed to helping out fellow sister guilds as well as protecting our home land, Gavel, especially Canyon.

Whats does Aesir do?

- Aesir is dedicated to helping its sister guilds, being able to cover their enemies from invasions is essential. Aesir guilds are effective in war and especially with keeping each others guilds secure. We are especially dedicated to Gavel, and the birth place of Aesir, the Canyon. ​

- Aesir guilds also have had events and are in the process of planning more, such as CSSTs, Giveaways, pictures, even events taking place outsides of wynn for more bonding. ​

- Members of Aesir are willing to help any of their allies with quests, dungeons, builds, anything so their allies can benefit. ​

Actual Origins:

.SpoilerTarget"> Spoiler: Origin Story
Last year during the peak of the War Syndicate alliance, Fux and SDU became friends with one another. Both were settled in the canyon as well as fought against invaders. The duo was split sadly during the Coalition phase, Fux entering Coalition and SDU warring as a rebel guild against Coalition. Fux decided to leave Coalition, and then became close with SDU again. When Coalition was taking its toll, Fux and SDU were offered to join a new alliance together, The Federation. After accepting, we ran into a guild that we grew fond of, IBT, IceBlue Team. Being a gavel owning guild and kind to Aesir, they found a home with Aesir. Today, Aesir stands happily hosting community events, helping its sister guilds, and making new friends. The next guild to be added was DiamondDeities, a friendly guild we have met in federation. This guild got every single to approve of them, earning their home in Aesir.

The Story Behind Aesir:

.SpoilerTarget"> Spoiler: Aesir's Lore
Long ago, when a single entity owned the land, rebels banded together to fight for their salvation and prosperity. The rebels fought day after day, with sleepless nights, only finishing when justice was earned. While the battle was fierce and violent, the leaders searched for something more than bloodshed and victory, for beauty, appreciation, and peace of the land.

As the Fierce war rumbled, the rebels of Fantasy and Sins of Seedia stumbled upon a beautiful, sacred land, with hidden passages, towering mountains, and seemingly boundless skies. Typically a subtle, yet treacherous canyon would prove too much for any normal Adventurer. However, It seemed instead of becoming lost within in this land, they found it was their home, bringing them what they longed for in the midst of war.

Months later, the battle ended, the entity that once ruled the land was vanquished, the rebels had proven victorious. With their newly found love for the Canyon, Fantasy and Sins of Seedia protected the canyon from invaders, becoming more than allies, but forming a special friendship between their guilds.

The guilds made their close friendship official, by creating the Aesir Pact. The pact swore they would take priority in protecting their canyon, but as well as fighting for what they believe is right, and protecting other guilds who became allies or even to enter Aesir someday.
