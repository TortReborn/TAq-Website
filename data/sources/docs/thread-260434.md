Eden was created in 2018 and has a deep history, both in guild politics and community relations.

It is a vibrant guild that has had a lot of ups and downs in the years it has been active. Our goal is to be a strong,

independent force in Wynncraft politics and have a strong standing in guild relations.

We also would like to stay a fun and welcoming guild to everyone.

We welcome people from all corners of the world and regularly host community events for our members.

​

[image: cropped rules.png]

We are not a strict guild, but please keep in mind our basic morals if you would like to apply:

1. Be welcoming and friendly towards everyone.

2. We expect you not to ask for promotions, you will get a promotion once you are worthy.

3. No forms of toxicity are tolerated. This even extends to outside of the guild and our community.

4. You need to be an active member of Wynncraft. If you take a break, you can do

that in a limited amount by telling us on our Discord,

but if you unexpectedly go inactive for more than 14 days, you will get kicked.

5. We expect you not to share any internal guild information with anyone outside of the guild.

[image: cropped hierarchy.png]

Now that we got the basics out of the way, let me tell you a bit about our guild (further referred to as Empire ).

We have a custom hierarchy system, so in-game ranks are just “placeholders”, if you will.

Institutions

Our Empire consists of three main institutions. Besides them, we also have smaller groups, e.g. for event management.

The Agora:

All Empire members are part of the Agora. It is where discussions and votes regarding all members happen,

and the votes here are made to get public opinion, rather than to make a concrete decision.

The Legion:

This institution is our military force, which keeps peace in our empire’s territories on the map.

Every member of the Empire can join the Legion by applying for an army rank.

The Senate:

This is an institution that the Consuls, Duxes, and Senates are a part of.

The Senate discusses and votes on decisions that largely impact the empire and its members.

Ranks

Most of the ranks in our hierarchy system can be obtained by anyone. The requirements to get ranks largely consist of being active

in-game & on our Discord server, warring, and being an active and contributing member of our society.

✮✮✮✮✮ Emperor:

Eden’s leader is the Emperor, who’s primary power is that they can veto votes the Senate passes. Other than that,

the Senate handles decision making. One can only become an Emperor if the current Emperor steps down and hands over their powers.

✮✮✮✮ Consul:

The two Consuls are the leaders of the Senate. They are representatives of the Empire who handle foreign relations,

have the power to start votes, and execute the decisions made by the Senate.

While one Consul is permanent and can only be replaced by them stepping down, the other one must be elected

by the Senate to work as the second Consul for one month. Only members of the Senate can be elected as Consuls.

✮✮✮✮ Dux:

The Dux are highly acknowledged members of the Senate who actively participate in the decisions of the empire

and have a large influence within the Senate. They are usually high-ranked military officers with authority just below the Consuls.

A Dux is chosen by the Senate for their outstanding contributions to the Empire.

✮✮✮ Senate:

Senates are the lowest ranked members of the Senate. They actively participate in and give critique to decisions made by the Empire.

They are chosen by the Senate based on both their military accomplishments and their competence in reasoning and judgement.

✮✮ Legate:

Legates are decorated soldiers who have earned their rank by participating in wars and being active in-game.

They are expected to act on their own in wars and to feel a sense of responsibility to the Empire. Legates are picked and voted on by the Senate.

✮ Legionnaire:

Legionnaires are trusted members who have shown an interest in warring and have participated in a good number of them.

Every citizen can be promoted to a Legionnaire by any member of the Senate without voting.

Citizen:

Citizens are usually new members, who will get promoted to higher ranks as they find their place in the Empire and help our community flourish.

They can take parts in Empire related tasks & events, they will get a promotion based on their contributions.
