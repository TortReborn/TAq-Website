As for Wynncraft on the whole, the Wynn province’s soundtrack (excluding the Silent Expanse) has been updated to use new instruments. Additionally, a new theme for the Parasite boss in Orphion’s Nexus of Light was introduced, called “Tip The Scales!”.
The Warrior’s Shield Strike ability from the Paladin archetype has also been reworked, so using Bash now sends broken mantles (or charges from the Mantle of the Bovemists ability) to an enemy, dealing damage to other nearby enemies. The Mage’s Winded status effect from the Riftwalker archetype has also been adjusted, with the extra damage only applying up to your personal max Winded stacks.
Alongside the ability changes, a new QoL feature has been added for gathering professions - a new experience bar for each profession that will show when you’re holding the specific tool for that profession.
[image: Prof XP Bar] Photo Credit: HeyZeer0
Oh, and not to mention that Wynncraft now has a “dupe bounty” program, where if you find a duplication glitch, or “dupe” you can claim up to 50 silverbull shares (roughly $200 USD, as of writing this) depending on the severity, and other factors. Do note that you won’t be banned if you report it properly, and if you are honest on how it was used. However, you must be the first to have reported said dupe, you must not have shared with anyone, and it must be reproducible and only used for testing purposes (Better think twice about using it to sell another un-ID’d Hero you got from a lootrun!). You would only receive the reward(s) after the dupe has been patched, so you will have to be patient!
And that brings us to the end of recent happenings! It's been a pleasure for me to inform you about what’s been going on in ANO and Wynncraft. With all that said, it’s time for me to hand over to my fellow writers.

The first event was hosted by none other than our goat Overfears - Bingo! By now surely everyone knows what bingo entails, but for those living under a rock who do not, bingo is just a game where players mark off prompts as they are called out, aiming to complete a row to win. Overfears offered two types. One was regular bingo where players only needed to get five in a row to win, with the other being “Lock out” which is essentially the same as regular bingo, but once one team collects an item no one else can collect it. There were three games and teams (maximum 4) had to earn “points” to see who came out on top. Truly peak gaming if I do say so myself.

Main Stage: Predictions for Fruma
Written by Lamplesspost
Hi everyone! I’m Lamplesspost and welcome once again to the main stage of Titan Times. With Fruma right around the corner, it’s time for me to make some predictions about the upcoming province, so without further ado, let us begin!
What we know already
Before we get to the predictions, let’s discuss what we already know about Fruma, from already existing lore within the game and from what leaks we’ve gotten so far. As of writing this, we know that Fruma is the origin of the player, as well as the Corkians. It’s been extremely isolationist for roughly 1000 years, and denies any outsiders access. Despite this, it has been sending recruits to Wynn to fight the Corruption since 900AP (100 years ago). These recruits seem to be “criminals” from a large prison in Fruma, probably near to the border with Wynn.
The province is ruled by a tyrannical queen, with the Sovereigns serving under her, probably as governors for specific areas. The latest Fruma sneak peek is titled The Sixth Sovereign, so we can most likely assume that there will be at least 6 of them, if not more. There also seems to be a large class divide between rich and poor in some areas, as we see in several Recover the Past flashbacks that there is a slum district, contrasting with what looks to be a more luxurious city, along with the imposing grandeur of the revamped Royal Gate south of Corkus.
There also seems to be some form of resistance against the Fruman government as well, with a graffitied poster in the background of an Espren sneak peek. The poster itself appears to have originally read “Join the Fight” so perhaps Fruma is involved in some sort of conflict. We also see poorer citizens in the slums stealing parts from factories, which implies a few things: firstly, Fruman technology is advanced enough to the point of them having factories, and secondly, the Fruman citizens aren’t particularly fond of the Fruman government.
[image: /_next/image?url=%2Fnews%2Fvol19%2F10.png&w=3840&q=75]
Additionally, we know a group of refugees fled Fruma around 350 years ago, becoming the Corkian people. This to me is more fairly solid evidence that not all is sunshine and rainbows in Fruma. I’d say something reasonably bad must be going on to convince a whole group of people to make what seems like a perilous journey to a completely unknown area of the world. It’s also interesting to note that the mountains around Fruma might have some strange magical properties, as the Corkians were reported to lose all their memories of the province when crossing through them, and all recruits sent to Wynn experience amnesia when travelling between the two provinces.
Moving over to the gameplay side of things, Fruma is confirmed to be an endgame area, and the level cap is set to increase, though not beyond 120. The CT has also told us that Fruma will be, unlike the mountainous Silent Expanse, very open, meaning hopefully there’ll be plenty of content to explore. And speaking of content, let’s get on to the predictions!
Content Predictions
So with all that in mind, what are my predictions for the actual content of Fruma? I think it’s pretty safe to say there’s going to be at least 1 new raid (maybe 2 if the CT spoils us) but what exactly will it be centred around? A personal theory of mine is that the aforementioned prison might be a raid, as it’s a suitably large and significant part of Fruma. The main rooms would consist of freeing prisoners and battling guards, and in my opinion more puzzles to solve would be a great addition to raids.
The boss could be some kind of prison warden, maybe one of the Sovereigns, or perhaps even the prison itself is a sentient enemy forged from dark magic.
It’s also possible that we could find the Fruman Queen herself inside a raid as the final boss, though I think it’s more likely she’ll be the boss of a dungeon similar to the Eye in Eldritch Outlook. My personal bet is that the dungeon/raid she’s in will involve us storming some giant fortress single handedly, ending with an epic battle in the throne room to decide the fate of the province.
[image: /_next/image?url=%2Fnews%2Fvol19%2F11.png&w=3840&q=75]
It’s also pretty likely there’s going to be a lootrun camp somewhere in Fruma to keep the lootrunners satiated, so hopefully we’ll see some cool new mechanics or maybe even a new category of items or something (offhands would be pretty sick).
I’m also hoping we get more major world events like Annie, but maybe instead of a 3 day timer you have to buy a key instead so Wynncraft players can get a sleep schedul- who am I kidding that’s never going to happen.
Maybe we could also see world events that consist of more than just killing mobs, perhaps puzzles or hunts or something along those lines. It would also be nice if we could see some new boss altars, maybe even a whole new type like guild boss altars, requiring you to team up with your guildmates to take down an even greater challenge in return for better loot.
It’s also pretty reasonable to assume we’re going to be getting new caves, secret discoveries and questlines, along with a new rune (Ek). I think personally it would be cool if we could get much larger, more dangerous caves that function almost like mini dungeons, as caves at the moment are a solid part of the game to get some quick loot early on, but not all that exciting otherwise.
Something else I would love to see added to Wynncraft is some form of endless mode content, perhaps requiring you to team up with 5-6 players to take on wave after wave of mobs, maybe with a boss every 10 or so levels. This could provide a new easily-repeatable way to gain LE and other forms of loot aside from raids and lootrunning.
[image: /_next/image?url=%2Fnews%2Fvol19%2F12.png&w=3840&q=75]
But let’s not forget there’s also the potential that we could get a new class! While I do think the 5 classes we have at the moment are pretty solid and definitely enough, I would certainly be intrigued to see what the CT manages to cook up. As for the classes we already have, I think it’s likely we’ll see more ability points along with brand new abilities to help us combat ever increasing threats; the CT has managed to come up with some incredible abilities recently so my hopes are high!
Lore/Story Predictions
Content aside, I also have plenty of predictions for what the story and lore of Fruma could look like. I think it’s fairly reasonable to say that Tasim will likely be an instrumental part of the main Fruma storyline, seeing how his ties to the province were highlighted in the revamped Recover the Past quest.
Unfortunately for Tasim, however, I have a personal theory that our good friend is going to meet his demise inside of Fruma, most likely before the player faces the Fruman Queen, leaving us to face her alone. As for how I think throughout his travels in Fruma, Tasim will begin to fully remember his past, leading to him becoming conflicted and unsure of if he’s doing the right thing, or of who he actually is. Then at some point we’ll encounter a formidable threat that the player is unable to properly deal with (maybe some immortal magical construct created by the Fruman Queen), and Tasim will finally realise who he truly is- a soldier of Wynn, before sacrificing himself heroically, allowing the player to escape.
Will it happen like that? Most likely not, though I’m still fairly convinced he’ll meet his end somehow. But why exactly am I even rooting for poor Tasim’s death? The honest answer is that it just feels fitting. A tragic sacrifice would have far more of an emotional impact on the story of the game than Tasim just becoming Fruma’s new governor or something, and give the player that extra drive to defeat the final boss, just like Aledar’s sacrifice gets you pumped up to kick the crap out of the Eye at the end of the Silent Expanse questline.
[image: /_next/image?url=%2Fnews%2Fvol19%2F13.png&w=3840&q=75]
It’s also just more compelling in my opinion to have the player leave Fruma alone, and therefore face our final challenges in Dern or wherever else alone. I just think whatever happens in Dern will be far more impactful if the player is forced to face it alone, with no friends or allies. But I digress, these are Fruma predictions not Dern predictions (though by all means I’d be willing to write that sometime in the future when we know a little more).
Anyway, as for the player’s story, I do have a theory for where that might go, although it might be considered a little out there as there isn’t very much evidence for it and it’s simply pure speculation. In Recover the Past, there’s a line that always stuck with me when the player tries to use the memory orb, something which I think is absolutely intentional. “It's almost like we were accessing memories that didn't exist.” This to me heavily suggests that the player is not from Fruma at all. This would be revealed by the fact that, unlike Tasim, the player never regains their memories of Fruma, because they never had any. They simply never existed because the player was never there. While I am a little biased, I do believe this theory has potential to come true. As for where the player’s true origin lies however, I must admit I don’t really have any ideas. In all honesty, it’s quite likely we won’t even find that out in Fruma.
Regarding the general lore of Fruma, I don’t have any particular theories per say. I think the Fruman Queen will likely have access to some kind of immortality, and will have been ruling for centuries. I don’t really have an explanation as to why besides it just feels like an evil monarch sort of thing to do and the fact that it would make her seem considerably more threatening and considerably more oppressive. Speaking of subjugation, I also think the large class divide between the rich and poor mentioned earlier will play an important role in Fruma’s main storyline.
And while we're roughly on the topic of magic, due to Tasim being referred to by Aledar as “cursed” I think magic users will likely be considered abominations and heretics, and magic itself will be considered a blight upon Fruma. Magic users would be persecuted by the Fruman government, maybe even tracked down and killed by specialised mage hunters. This could potentially lead to some interesting conflicts with the player and Tasim, who are, after all, magic users. Despite this, though, I have my suspicions that the Fruman Royalty and the upper echelons of Fruman society such as the Sovereigns might secretly have access to magic themselves, and use it to maintain an iron grip over the Fruman people.

And with that we’ve reached the end of my predictions! I hope you found it interesting, and if you have any theories of your own I’d love to hear them. Thank you for reading, and I’ll see you all in the next edition!
