MAG - The Mage Legacy

So I just drew a mage dude. I guess I used the colors of their banner for some of the colors. Other than that, super fun to draw! I love drawing spell people. :>

AEQ - Aequitas

Okay, honestly I had a lot of fun with this. So their name is the Latin concept of justice, equality and I wanted to do something with Ancient Greek. So bam! Ancient-Greek-inspired design! Also, they are wearing a blindfold because "justice is blind" kinda thing.

CNM - ChinaNumberOne

My guild~! My lovely, lovely guild that is full of wonderful sweet, and scary people. I did an Ancient-China-inspired design with a modern twist. Red and black for the colors on the banner. The little ornament on his hip is a lemon because our discord is called lemon hub.

WFN - Where's The Finish

So their banner is a cash-money looking purple sword. Going off that, I wanted to do a soldier/assassin type with lots of gear. Think "Winter-Soldier"-ish but without the sad backstory.

TNL - TheNoLifes

From what my Guild Leader told me, they are the ones that know more about guild politics and all that cool stuff. And since the current Guild Leader of TNL has a steve skin, I thought yeah man that's like that old grizzled adventurer archetype so yea...

IBT - IceBlue Team

Don't be mad but I totally thought of a pokemon team when I read the name. Like you know Team Rocket or Team Aqua type of vibes. He's got that whole ice-maniac thing going on but I'm sure they are cool as heck. Get it? "Cool" as heck? hahaha!

Other Notes:

Please understand that this is just my doodles of what I think the Guilds would look like if they were people. It's not representative of them by any means and oh god maybe I should have asked permission but it's kinda like fanart I guess? If you don't like it, I'm sorry but remember it is just what I personally think they would look like. Either way, I hope the members of each guild like it, or at least it puts a smile on your face. I'm thinking of doing the other guilds in the goose alliance, what do you think? There's a lot more and I only did the ones that I was more familiar with so I'm sorry if your guild is not on here. Any specific requests?
