EDN - Eden

Yup. It's Eden, the big bois who own a whole lotta territory. Really cool guys but I get a military vibe from them since I see them helping out other guilds during wars a lot. (At least when I'm on) Also on top of them is a kiwi (yes the bird) as a little shout-out to @N1nj4_K1w1 since he is a part of EDN.

GsW - Guardian of Wynn

A mix of their flag colors + guardian-esqe character. I honestly debated putting a spike ball on the end of GsW's braid but that would probably be overkill. So I settled with a gladiator-style guardian!

cxz - Fuzzy Spiders

....sPIDeRs Their hair is the gradient of their flag. I haven't met too many people from their guild but they seem real nice. :)

SHY - ShadowFall

Purple boi-shadowman? Their banner has a purple center with white horns so I thought of bone demon man. There was a previous rendition of them just being a creature of shadow.

TNI - The Dark Phoenix

Emo dark phoenix? It's the first thing I thought of sorry haha! I gave him bird claws for feet but kinda stuck with a loose-clothing style for winged people.

AVO - Avicia

I know the AVO does not mean the avo people from Corkus but that's what I thought of? Plus, their territory is currently located in the Sky Islands so more wings = more flight (That's definitely not right lol). I'd like to think AVO and TNI are kinda bros just because they are both winged guilds personified.

Other Notes:

So like the other one, just fanart! Not meant to actually represent the guilds and just something to do in my spare time. I take requests from friends and the GOOSE alliance still has more I need to finish but please remember if I take the request, it will be my representation. I love suggestions but I really don't like when people tell me what to draw; it makes me a little uncomfortable. Sorry! My commissions are not open right now sorry!
