Dive into Adventure with The Aquarium [TAq]

Welcome to the Depths! Are you ready to take your Wynncraft experience to the next level? At The Aquarium [TAq], we're not just a guild; we're a thriving community of adventurers who share a passion for the game and community.

Ready to elevate your Wynncraft journey? Join The Aquarium [TAq], where we're more than just a guild – we're a vibrant community of passionate adventurers who share a deep love for the game and its community. As the highest-level guild at level 120, we're here to take your experience to the next level.

Our community is warm and lively, boasting 150 members who are ready to assist with any topic. At The Aquarium, you'll find a hub for learning and sharing insights on various aspects of the game, from warring and XP grinding to lootrunning and general gameplay.

When it comes to warring , The Aquarium is an ideal spot for skill enhancement, regardless of your experience level. Over the past year, we consistently secured either 1st or 2nd place in the Season leaderboard, a testament to our unwavering dedication and expertise.

Our monthly events are highly regarded for their excellence. Our dedicated build team puts in substantial effort to create unique and expertly crafted content, presenting Wynn or TAq-themed minigames that showcase custom dungeons, items, creatures, and abilities.

We are in search of members who aspire to master the game and can commit time on a weekly basis. We value active participation in both in-game and Discord conversations as the most crucial form of contribution, for which you'll receive rewards. Engaging in wars and XP grinding is also highly encouraged and comes with generous rewards, including promotions within the guild and direct LE rewards.

Our requirements are simple: you only need to possess a Discord account and have a Wynncraft class at level 80 or higher.

To become a part of our guild, please visit our stats page at https://wynncraft.com/stats/guild/The Aquarium to locate an online member who can provide you with a Discord invitation . Once you've joined our Discord server, navigate to the #applications channel and follow the instructions for a quick application process.

Please be aware that this forum thread isn't consistently monitored, so any applications or questions posted here may go unnoticed.

Join The Aquarium [TAq] and embark on a journey like no other. Be a part of history, be a part of the wave!
