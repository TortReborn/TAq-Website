For the first chapter of this story, I’ll begin with where my entire guild history started. HoL.
I had been in another guild before HoL, it’s true.
That guild was called Pirates of Wynn [PoW]
I joined it in early January 2015, when it was a top 10 guild constantly fighting Kangronomicon for the #6 rank. After only a week at most of being in this guild, I went on a hiatus from Wynncraft.
When I came back, it was late May 2015. I saw that the guild had died and left it. That very same day, while asking for help with the Tower of Ascension, I met Kuroi.
After he assisted me with ToA, he invited me to the Holders of LE [HoL]
The guild was in a poor state at the time. HoL’s reputation had never been lower. The guild had gone through multiple scandals, including a duping ordeal that got many members banned.
However, its members seemed blissfully unaware of the disdain much of the community held for them
The guild was owned by a player called SSEEBBYZ, or Seb for short.
Seb was highly inactive, and had given leadership of the guild to yosteric5.
I quickly established a dominant role within the guild and accomplished simple tasks that had been ignored, such as posting a guild thread.
Roughly a week in to being a member of HoL, I met with other members at the Troms throne room in what would later be named the “Secret HoL Illuminati Society” or SHIS for short.
At this meeting, myself and 3-4 other members discussed the state of the guild’s leadership and formulated possible plans to remove yosteric from the leadership position.
We ended up deciding on a simple plan: message Spham, the leader of Kangronomicon, and get her to speak to Seb about removing yosteric as the leader.
Kuroi and I were the ones who would execute the plan. We messaged Spham on the forums. Here’s a screenshot.
Image
Spham agreed to speak to him, but only if he logged on to Wynn to do it. This presented a dilemma, as he never played Wynn and neither of us knew his Skype.
Kuroi and I discussed the issue for a while, and finally decided that simply speaking to yos might prove to be more beneficial than we thought.
We approached him and made him aware of our grievances with the guild, and he agreed to make us both “Head Chiefs”
This role essentially gave us the power to run the guild in his absence and do what we saw fit.
As the weeks went on, HoL began to gain more respect. Kuroi and myself wanted to gain the friendship of Kangronomicon, so to prove ourselves we would organize regular skirmishes on TNA that in which we would frequently be able to take and hold about 6-7 territories before they pushed us back.
Back then, this was no small accomplishment. Kangronomicon [Fuq] and Nether’s Ascent [TNA] were essentially the Hax of mid 2015.
It was through these skirmishes that we were finally noticed and taken in by Spham as a subguild.
Shortly before this, there was a devastating glitch that made it where Fuq was unable to  attack. To counteract this and keep Fuq in power, HoL and the other subguilds were tasked with taking back any territories that were captured.
During the time of this, HoL far surpassed the other subguilds. We managed to take (and hold) 50 territories before we were finally struck with the same glitch that prevented us from attacking.
50 territories may not sound like a large amount, but this was back when dozens of guilds warred every day, and back before Gavel territories existed.
That amount was almost unheard of back then.
After being struck with the glitch, my mind shifted to other thoughts.
I suddenly became struck with the fear that yosteric would find out about the SHIS and kick me from the guild or demote me
By this point in time, I had been made Co-Leader of the Holders of LE, so I wanted to avoid that outcome at all costs.
I decided that the best course of action would be to message yosteric and tell him myself in an effort to avoid any harsh punishment.
So, I did.
Here’s the exact message I sent, all the way back in 2015
To my utter shock and horror, yosteric demoted me all the way down to recruit. I was Co-Leader of the guild and with a single message he made me a recruit.
I did not have the patience to work my way up the ranks and deal with similar issues again, so I applied to Kangronomicon.
When yosteric saw this, he kicked me from the guild. At this point there was no foreseeable hope of redemption in HoL, so as soon as I was accepted to Fuq, I fully dedicated myself to it.
Less than a week after joining, the glitch finally ended and Fuq could attack again.
That very night I helped take about 5 territories before having to sleep.
I woke up, and Fuq had every territory back. That included all the Ragni territories, all the Nivla ones, all the Detlas ones, all the Ternaves ones, and all the Elkurn ones. In one night.
Because of my constant help in Fuq, and the warring Spham had witnessed when I was in HoL, I quickly earned the rank of captain
I continued helping Fuq for a week or two to follow.
Then, I started speaking to yosteric again. He mentioned that HoL had been down on its luck ever since I had been kicked from the guild.
He gave me an offer: Come back to HoL, and you’ll have the Co-Leader rank again.
After consulting with Spham, I chose to rejoin HoL with my alt account.
For time reference, at this point in the story we’re at September 13th, 2015.
As soon as I joined, I laid down the one rule for while I was in the guild again: No attacking Kangronomicon
This was a reasonable request, as I was a member of Fuq and couldn’t be in two guilds that were warring against each other.
Less than a week later, yosteric began to accuse me of being a Fuq spy.
I didn’t understand this. He knew from the start I was a member of Fuq, and he was fully aware of my “no attacking Kangro” rule.
Yosteric again attempted to kick me from the guild. Since he was technically a chief in game such as myself, he couldn’t kick me.
He messaged SSEBBYZ and asked him to kick me, but Seb basically told him that we were both Co-Leaders and that neither one of us could kick the other.
We attempted to work it out, but it proved useless.
Then, I saw a way out. Seb confided in me that he wanted to sell the guild. I asked him how much and he replied with “5 stacks”.
I asked him to keep that a secret until I could acquire some funds, and he obliged.
I messaged a few people to ask for money. One of these people was Spham. She said that she wouldn’t give me money, but that she’d get her boyfriend, unicorn67, to buy HoL for her.
I was upset with this due to the fact that I wouldn’t gain ownership of the guild, but I settled.
We hyped it up. We told the guild that we were going to have an important announcement on September 22nd.
The day came, and everyone logged on to Wynn to hear the announcement.
Seb told everyone that he was going to give the guild to his “cousin”, AALLEEXX.
This was obviously just Spham’s alt account.
He left the guild and gave it to Spham. The first thing she did was demote yosteric to recruit, and announce that she would be inactive and that I would be the new leader of HoL. She gave me her alt account’s password so that I could promote and demote Chiefs at my own discretion.
I had planned to leave yosteric as a chief. I wasn’t that cruel. I knew he had contributed to the guild and didn’t want to strip him of his achievements.

Hello everyone! I'll make this short, but here's a tale I remember of one of the few times Ordo's might was tested by a single guild.
The Uprising of TheAwakening
Over a month after it’s inception, it was a quiet and chilly day for Ordo Malleus. Every guild was performing its daily routine of taking care of its designated territories. Long after the fall of Concordia, the alliance members turned their focus upon other matters different than guild politics, such as the coming of the long awaited Gavel Province. No one had suspected there to be any threat to arise for the great alliance.
They struck at night. A new guild, TheAwakening, began its foothold in Almuj and the desert, taking HackForum’s designated area. At first, we didn’t think them as much of a threat, and left Hax to clean its house of intruders. However, the leaders of Ordo Malleus began to worry as Hax could not hold its own. By each day, TheAwakening’s presence grew larger in the desert. By now, the leaders began planning an assault on TheAwakening in order to push them back and help Hax regain its footing.
When word came out of the plans of the assault to every alliance member, I rallied the war captains of my guild. Back then, I was just the chief and co-leader of ToastedPandas. But even so, I was essentially treated as a leader, and thus allowed to know of the attack beforehand. As our horses galloped us from Detlas to Almuj, we prepared ourselves for hours of fighting under the name of both our guild ToastedPandas and for Ordo Malleus.
As we arrived, we noticed the ensuing battles of Hax and Awk (TheAwakening). I messaged DarkSouls, the leader of Hax, in order to coordinate our attacks. The plan would be for both of us to attack Awk, but TnP would leave minuscule defenses so that Hax could take its territories back twice as fast as Awk could take them. This tactic worked, and both Hax and TnP were able to push Awk back.
For a bit.
The next day, TheAwakening began gaining traction again, starting in Almuj just as they had before.
