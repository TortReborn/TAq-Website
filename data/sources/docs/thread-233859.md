Avicia is a community and war guild and we actively look for people who would fit into one or preferably both of those niches. While we do accept people of all levels of experience, we are more likely to accept the applications of people who have higher-leveled war-able classes or people who we believe will be able to make significant contributions to the community of Avicia. That being said, we recommend that you have at least one level 80+ class before you apply and that you anticipate being active at least a couple of hours a day several days a week.

To apply, we highly recommend filling out an application on our guild Discord server; doing so will ensure the fastest response. You can also fill out an application via this Google Forms link:

https://forms.gle/rkZo7dtKmuMsMnbK6

If you would prefer, you may apply via this thread instead. Please do not change the format of the application or it will be automatically denied. We usually respond to applications within 48 hours, though that time may vary depending on the availability of space in the guild.
