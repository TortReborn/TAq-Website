Welcome to the Lux Imperius!

Lux Imperius is an alliance founded by @xMC_Master11x , @motoki1 , @BlackbirdMC , @Loxotus , and @Arkam initially for war and community purposes. Lux Imperius is now a community alliance that values honor, prestige, and respect. The alliance sometimes has fun and serious events together.

Current Guilds:

Click on a guild's name to view their thread.

Dragon's Arise (DsA) :

Dragon's Arise is a prestigious community guild that strives among the strongest guilds out there. Founded in July of 2016 by @xMC_Master11x , the guild offers Wynncraft players a chance to be a part of a helpful community, where respect is prioritized. The guild also gives the opportunity to compete with other guilds. Sometimes, guild leaders will host events and ceremonies in honor of our companions, and their achievements. We have fun playing with and helping out our guildmates, and competing with other guilds.

UltimateXeons (UXs) :

UltimateXeons is a highly ranked guild, founded by @motoki1 ,

and it is a Community Guild (Also Semi-War).

Players in this guild are friends with each other and treat others as a community.

Eternal Legacy (ETL) :

Eternal Legacy is a one year guild leading up a crew of high leveled members since February the 27th of 2015 by its amazing friendly leaders @Commer and the former leader @nicedoge509 with the help of the loyal veteran chiefs whom are considered as owner's right hand and are excited to genuinely answer any proposed question

We always had the aim to level up our guild with the help of our members, and proudly being on our 40s (2 stars), we still believe on this desire, our next aim is to reach top 10, with your help it's possible

WarLords (War) :

WarLords is a frech guild that is motivated for war, and determined to be their best. It is a great community, and it is lead by @Arkam

GrimNation (GrN) :

Grim Nation, aka [GrN], is a young, ambitious guild aiming to become a fighting force. Currently very

active, we also hope to become closer to one another and make new friends! From

helping out other members to warring, we hope to have you join our ranks as we

climb our way to the top! This guild is led by @MagicGum .

Beasts (BST):

Beasts is a rather new, but rapidly leveling guild. Led by @Beastovrlrd , Beasts is thriving in wars and is happy to join the Lux Imperius.

Joining Lux Imperius:

Currently, joining the Lux Imperius is closed. We will tell you as soon as we open it up for applications.
