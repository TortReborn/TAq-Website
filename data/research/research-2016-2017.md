# 2016–2017 guild-politics dossier (pre-territory-log era)

Research pass 2026-09-02. Standard per chronicle-research-notes: **ATTESTED** = named, dated, written
source (thread number + post date + author + quote). **[image]** = the community guild-timeline image
(imgur seklYAQ, posted by DerpyEmerald24 in thread 265297, Apr 3 2020) — a *recollection compiled in
2020*, dated entries but not contemporaneous; treat as its own provenance tier. **RECOLLECTION** =
memory-thread posts years later. **INFERENCE** clearly labeled.

slice0-5.png in this scratchpad). Its 2014–2017 entries are transcribed in §8.

## 1. Valkyrie's early years (2015–2017) — now well attested

Three successive alliance threads recovered:

### Thread 89529 — "⌠ Valkyrie Alliance ⌡" (the FOUNDING thread)
- **Sep 10, 2015**, by **LoveLusting**, titled "Queen of the Foxes" (Kingdom of Foxes).
- Founding date stated: **Jun 2, 2015**, between **Kingdom of Foxes** and **Imperial**.
- "not established for the purpose of war … solidifying social relations between the two guilds."
- Governance: the "**Valkyrie Electorate**" — guild leaders deciding foreign affairs/policy by
  "unanimous agreement in their decision-making process."
- **Lightly Toasted Heroes** joined by **Dec 12, 2015** (announced at a seasonal award ceremony);
  leaders named **Klove** and **Emily**.
- Named figures: LoveLusting (KoF), **Yuno F Gasai** (Imperial, "Imperial Family").

### Thread 147790 — "Alliance ⌠ Valkyrie Alliance ⌡" (relaunch #2)
- **Aug 29, 2016**, by **Yuno F Gasai** (Imperial).
- Members listed: Imperial, Kingdom of Foxes, **House of Sentinels**, **Lightly Toasted Heroes** [Lth].
- Governance here called the "**Valkyrie Parliament**": "All powers regarding the conclusion of
  foreign affairs or policy will be forfeited to the Valkyrie Parliament … guild masters of each
  member-guild … unanimous agreement."
- Leadership chain, Yuno F Gasai, Sep 2016: "**ahri left so Kv had to make a new one, then Kv left
  so now I run this thing**" — i.e. Valkyrie thread-keeper chain ahri → Kv → Yuno F Gasai.
- Reply banter: "pretzule leads a Valkyrie guild tho" (Pretzule = House of Sentinels, see §5).

### Thread 159734 — "⌠ Valkyrie Alliance ⌡" (relaunch #3, the Drew1011 era begins)
- **Oct 15, 2016**, by **Drew1011**.
- Members at relaunch: **Kingdom of Foxes, Holders of LE, Ha Ha United** [HHU]. **Imperial had
  departed by this date**; "Lightly Toasted had also recently left the alliance to attempt guild
  revival." Reply by DireFire, Oct 15 2016: "R.I.P OG Valkyrie guilds".
- Same Parliament text reused ("forfeited to the Valkyrie Parliament", unanimous).
- Matches Drew1011's profile title to the day: "Leader of Valkyrie Alliance: 15 October 2016 —
  10 March 2018" (see §6).

**Correction candidate for the chronicle:** Valkyrie governance is attested under two names —
"Electorate" (2015 OP) and "Parliament" (2016 OPs). Membership beyond Fox+Imp now attested:
House of Sentinels + Lightly Toasted Heroes (2016), Holders of LE + HaHaUnited (Oct 2016), with
Imperial OUT between Aug 29 and Oct 15, 2016.

Kingdom of Foxes tension: [image] says "July 2nd 2016 KoF is disbanned [sic]" and "December 3 2016
Fox is revived", yet thread 159734 (Oct 15 2016) lists Kingdom of Foxes as a member. Either a partial
revival predated Dec 3, or the OP listed KoF aspirationally. Unresolved; both sources recorded.

  probably **the biggest event that happened in 2017**".

### The name "Hax & Co"
**DEAD END:** the phrase "Hax & Co"/"Hax and Co" appears in NO indexed forum thread, the timeline
image, or any archived page I could reach. Contemporary evidence shows Hax + unnamed allies
("2 anti hax alliances" implies a pro-Hax bloc), but the bloc's *name* is unattested in writing.
Treat "Hax & Co" as community shorthand of unknown origin until a source surfaces.

### How Hax fell (the biggest event of 2017) — still mostly unwritten
No thread recounting the mechanics of the 2017 fall was found. What the record supports:
- Hax >300 terrs May 2017 → Coalition takes Wynn Province Nov 23 2017 (Timeline doc, already in
  chronicle) → Hax holds only 111 territories by Feb 19 2018 (Fed wipe, [image]).
- INFERENCE (label as such): the 350→111 collapse happened in the Oct–Dec 2017 Coalition-era wars;
  the [image] "350 territories are wiped" entry sits in exactly that gap. No written account names
  the attackers, dates, or sequence.
