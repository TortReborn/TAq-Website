## 1. The chroniclers — who wrote the record down

### Sg_Voltage — creator and maintainer of the Guild Megalist ⚠ CORRECTION
- **[ATTESTED]** The megalist (thread 278112, "Read Before Applying To A Guild - All Currently Recruiting Guilds Megalist") was created by **Sg_Voltage** on **Nov 29, 2020** — *not* Pally as our notes assumed. First post: "I've assembled a list of every* guild which is currently recruiting along with some important stats… My goal is to be able to make sure every guild gets the chance to shine." Source: live thread p1.
- **[ATTESTED]** Forum profile (Wayback capture 20210704072714 of thread 278112, saved as scratchpad/megalist-20210704.html): CHAMPION rank, Minecraft name Sg_Voltage, **no guild field displayed** — his guild remains unknown. Custom title: "1.18 was the best update, don't @me".
- **[ATTESTED]** Crowd-edit model: "If you're interested in editing your guilds info on this list… send me a message with your Gmail and I'll give you editing permission" (OP, same capture) — this Google Sheet is why the alliance rosters are lost.

- **[ATTESTED]** Post #161, **Jul 7, 2022**: "I'm just going to delete that section. If it's not updated regularly it's not really useful anyways." — the end of public alliance record-keeping (thread p9).
- Player page: strong candidate — the era's registrar.

## 4. Political stories with names

### ShadowFall's GoulartJJ: Artemis leader → Goose (Mar 2020 → Feb 2021)
- **[ATTESTED]** Thread 263975 = "**The Artemis Alliance And Why It's Problematic To Me**" by **VeeeKoooN** (WrathOfTheFallen), 9 Mar 2020 — itself a political document: describes the predecessor **Luna** alliance and "the Great War," small guilds burned out defending big guilds' land.
- **[ATTESTED]** Post #36 (p2), 9 Mar 2020, **GoulartJJ**: "as the Shadow Fall leader and one of Artemis leaders I'd like to say that small guilds…" — direct, dated proof of Shy's Artemis leadership seat. ⚠ His forum badge at capture time reads "Imperial" — either later guild movement or badge lag; the post text is the authority.
- **[ATTESTED]** Post #3 same day, **Robturne** (Kingdom Foxes): "I'm an Artemis member, not really a higher up or anything, but if you're not in the alliance you're not restricted from attacking any given territory." **Asthae** (Eden) posted at #15 as an involved party — Eden brushing against Artemis 9 months before joining the coalition against it.
- **[ATTESTED]** The switch: "BNU(IBT), SHY and TNL are all goose now" — urbymine (AVO), 1 Feb 2021 (278112 p4).
- Epilogue: ShadowFall **won guild Season 1** (Jul 9 – Sep 20, 2021) — see §5 — then joined Niflheim (dissolved 18 Dec 2021), and by 2023 the name survived as a TAq subguild. Full arc = flagship player/guild-page story.

### The Goose fanart roster (Mar 30 / Apr 6, 2021)
- Threads 289196 + 289556 by **Drasalia** (CNM) — see §1. Twelve guilds drawn: CNM, Aeq, WFN, Mag, TNL, IBT + EDN, GsW, CXZ, Shy, TNI, AVO; explicitly partial. The only visual artifact of any alliance roster in the era.

### The Eden coup — thin but real
- **[ATTESTED]** Sole written source stands: **xK6TA**, ANO thread 206094 **page 97, post #1922, Apr 16, 2023**: "I was a part of Eden when it was #1 and slightly after it got couped." (exact post number/date recovered this pass).
- **[FINDING]** Eden **never won a guild season** (full table §5) — so "when it was #1" cannot mean a season crown; it most plausibly means #1 on the all-time guild leaderboard/warcount or peak-Khaos map standing (Eden held #1 leaderboard spots in the 2021 Khaos era per IamFye's "#14 and up are the mega alliance guilds" landscape, but no document says "Eden #1" directly). Coup date: before Apr 2023, otherwise unbounded.
- Searched: Eden thread 260434 (application traffic only, no politics), forum-wide coup searches. **No second source exists.** The coup's actors are unrecoverable from public text.

### Gang of Fools' Season-8 betrayal (autumn 2022)
- **[ATTESTED]** TT v2 (23 May 2022): FOOL as enemy ("failed reclaim attempts"); v3 (12 Jun 2022): FOOL joins Cucumber Company; v6 (12 Oct 2022): "Gang of Fools [FOOL] has left and betrayed the alliance."
- **No individual FOOL leaders are named in any issue.** Six-month arc enemy→ally→betrayer is fully dated but personless. DEAD END on names.

### Khaos leadership (WheresTheFinish / ChinaNumberOne / Fuzzy Spiders; TNL/EDN)
- **[RECOLLECTION only]** TNL/EDN leading Khaos rests entirely on Cameron's testimony (already in the draft). Fresh searches for "Khaos" + guild names produced **zero further written sources**; the name Khaos still has exactly one written attestation (Dream, 25 May 2021). Confirmed DEAD END: Khaos's internal structure was never written down publicly.

### Profession Heaven's neutrality (25 Jan 2021)
- **[ATTESTED]** PROF founded 20 Jan 2021; founder **Crokee**; thread 285237 (9 Feb 2021) codifies neutrality as internal law: "Keep the guild as neutral as possible towards all other guilds. No bias towards any guild."
- The **25 Jan 2021 Goose+Artemis protection pact brokers are not named in the PROF thread or anywhere indexed**. The pact date survives only via the Timeline doc already in the DB. DEAD END on broker names — candidates for Discord-side testimony: Crokee (PROF), Goose/Artemis leads.

### The moderation crisis of Dec 2020 (new event candidate)
- Thread 279337 (Sheepn, DXI, 18 Dec 2020) — the Artemis-vs-coalition war spilling into a legitimacy fight over guild-affiliated moderators banning enemy warrers. Named voices: Sheepn (DXI), IceResistance (ANO), Carrie (PUN), Miles_ (ANO). This is the best-documented political flashpoint between the war's ignition (Dec 2020) and Goose's naming (Jan 2021); consider a chronicle event "The moderator dispute" with these four names.
