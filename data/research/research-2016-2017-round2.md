# 2016–2017 dossier, round 2 — attacking the round-1 holes

Research pass 2026-09-03. Companion to `research-2016-2017.md` (round 1); **does not supersede it**.
Same provenance tiers: **ATTESTED** = named, dated, written, contemporaneous source. **RETROSPECTIVE
(written)** = written source composed after the fact but by a participant. **[image]** = the 2020
community timeline PNG. **RECOLLECTION** = memory-thread posts. **INFERENCE** labelled.

**Method that broke the deadlock.** Round 1 used forum keyword search + a Wayback CDX sweep of thread
slugs. Both are blind to threads whose *titles* don't contain the keyword. This pass instead
**enumerated the guild subforum itself**. The Wynncraft guild subforum is `forums/guilds.72/`, and
the live forum accepts `?order=post_date&direction=asc&page=N`, which lists every thread ever posted
there in creation order, 20 per page, 108 pages. It also accepts `?prefix_id=82`, the **"Alliance"**
thread prefix. Two things followed immediately:

1. A **complete census of every Alliance-prefixed thread in Wynncraft history** — exactly 40 threads,
   two pages. Archived as `forums-wynncraft-com-forums-guilds-72-prefix-id-82-order-post-date-direction-asc`
   and `…-page-2-…`.
2. A **complete title census of guilds.72 for Jan 2016 – Jun 2018** (pages 30–60), which surfaced
   three threads no keyword search would ever have found, including the one that closes hole #2.

Note also: threads 179383, 252647, 265297, 219744 are all in guilds.72. Guild politics did **not**
spill into Wynncraft Discussion. So the guilds.72 census is effectively a census of the whole
guild-political forum record, which makes the negative results below unusually strong.

- **vised**, thread **252647** post #5, **Jul 21 2019**: "Luna is just another mega-alliance. There
  have been quite a few throughout Wynn's history, like **Federation, Hax & Co, Coalition** and so

  retrospective (2019, about 2016–17) but it is written, dated, and by a guild-community regular,

## HOLE 2 — How Hax fell. **CLOSED, and it was NOT a 2017 event.**

### The thread
**Thread 219744 — "Haxforums Has Fallen At Last."**, **Femboy Orphion**, **Feb 22 2018 at 5:24 PM**:

> "After years of being number 1, **HaxForums has lost all its territories**. Now its much easier to
> be on the leaderboard, since the guilds with the most territories have only **43**. Everyone
> celebrate!"

Two replies, then closed by moderator **Pepo** ("This thread isn't relevant. /Thread closed.") four
minutes later. That is why no search ever found it and why no discussion of the mechanics survives
on the forums: **the one thread about the fall of Hax was locked before anyone could discuss it.**

### What this settles
- **Date of the fall: on or just before Feb 22 2018.** Not Oct–Dec 2017 as round 1 inferred.
- It **fits the Federation's founding to the week**: Federation founded **Feb 16 2018** (Drew1011
  profile / [image]); [image] "Fed wipe" leaves Hax at **111 territories Feb 19 2018**; Hax at
  **zero** by Feb 22 2018. The collapse from 111 to 0 took **three days**.
- The "43 territories" line also dates the post-Hax map: **no guild held more than 43 territories**
  immediately after, i.e. Hax's land was dispersed, not inherited by one successor.
- **Correction for the chronicle:** Lego_DW's "the fall of Hax … the biggest event that happened in
  2017" (thread 265297 #16, Apr 2020) is **off by about two months**. The *decline* was 2017 (>300
  terrs May 2017 → 111 by Feb 19 2018); the *fall* was **February 2018**, and the agent was the
  **Federation**, not the Coalition. Round 1's inference that "the 350→111 collapse happened in the
  Oct–Dec 2017 Coalition-era wars" is now superseded for the final act, though the Coalition-era
  attrition through late 2017 is still what got Hax down to 111.

### Corroborating narrative (RETROSPECTIVE, written)
**Thread 227373**, "The Aesir Pact", **lotuss**, mid-2018 — its lore section allegorises the whole
arc without naming Hax:

> "Long ago, when **a single entity owned the land**, rebels banded together to fight for their
> salvation and prosperity… Months later, the battle ended, **the entity that once ruled the land
> was vanquished, the rebels had proven victorious**."

## HOLE 3 — War Syndicate. Still no roster or leaders, but the surrounding record is now solid.

### The one substantive written source (RETROSPECTIVE) — round 1 had it archived but did not quote it
**Thread 227373**, "The Aesir Pact", **lotuss**, spoiler "Actual Origins":

> "**Last year during the peak of the War Syndicate alliance**, **Fux and SDU** became friends with
> one another. **Both were settled in the canyon** as well as fought against invaders. The duo was
> split sadly **during the Coalition phase, Fux entering Coalition and SDU warring as a rebel guild
> against Coalition**. Fux decided to leave Coalition, and then became close with SDU again. **When
> Coalition was taking its toll, Fux and SDU were offered to join a new alliance together, The
> Federation.**"

This is the only prose account of the WS → Coalition → Federation sequence written by a participant.
It yields:
- **Fantasy [Fux]** and **Sins of Seedia [SDU]** were both active, canyon-based guilds during the WS
  peak (late 2017), fighting "invaders" — consistent with, but not proof of, WS membership.
- **Fantasy [Fux] joined the Coalition. Sins of Seedia [SDU] fought the Coalition as a rebel guild.**
  These are the first two named guild-level alignments in the Coalition war.
- Fux later **left** the Coalition — the Coalition suffered defections before its end.
- Both were then recruited into the **Federation**.
- Aesir leaders named: **Viaire** and **SpaseCow** (Fux); **simplyinfinity_** and **Shy_Phoenix**
  (SDU); **Vmark03** and **Mpr100** (IBT); **Nitrogen2Oxygen** (DDT — the ex-Emperator of the
  Emperium of Wynn, so the Emperium leadership survived into the Federation era).

Chronology note: **Fantasy [Fux] was founded Jul 19 2017** (guild page, thread 249994) — **two days
after WS's creation date of 7/17/17** [image]. Suggestive, not probative.

### The WS remnant
[image] says "Illustratus is created from the remains of WS". **Illustratus [lus]** is listed as a
**member guild of the Federation** in thread **237070** ("Federation Tribute", Drew1011) — so the WS
remnant did end up inside the Federation, corroborating Lego_DW's version over the [image]'s garbled
Dec 24 2017 date. Illustratus's own 2019 revival thread (251505) has no WS history.

### Dead end, now much better evidenced
- The **complete Alliance-prefix census** (40 threads, all of Wynncraft history) contains **no War
  Syndicate thread**, under that or any abbreviation.
- The **complete guilds.72 title census for Jul 2017 – Jun 2018** (pages 47–60, ~280 thread titles)
  contains **no thread whose title mentions War Syndicate, WS, or any plausible variant**.
- Wayback CDX filtered across `*.wynncraft.com*` for any URL containing "syndicate": **zero results**.
- No `[WS]` tag appears anywhere in the recruiting megalist (thread 278112, all 9 pages).
- **Verdict: War Syndicate left no forum footprint of any kind.** It was Discord-native. Members and
  leaders remain UNRECOVERABLE from written sources. Only oral history will close this.

## HOLE 4 — The Coalition's founding. **Confirmed non-existent as a written source.**

- The **complete Alliance-prefix census** shows what the Coalition-era alliance threads actually
  were, and the Coalition is not among them. In creation order across 2017–2018:
  **188024** Emperium of Wynn (Paladin A'renos, May 7 2017) → **202167** *Amnesty* (Extriq, **Aug 30
  2017**, a German private clan page — despite the alliance prefix, not a war bloc) → **202724**
  Emperium of Wynn, second thread (Cloud Man, Sep 4 2017) → **224905** *Revolution* (Wynnooblet,
  **Apr 25 2018**) → **227373** The Aesir Pact (lotuss) → **236914/236962/237070** the Federation
  threads. **There is a clean gap where the Coalition should be: Sep 4 2017 → Apr 25 2018.**
- The **guilds.72 title census for Oct 2017 – Feb 2018** likewise contains no Coalition thread and no
  Nov 23 2017 Wynn-takeover thread.
- Wayback CDX across `*.wynncraft.com*` for URLs containing "oalition": **zero results**.
- **Verdict: DEAD END, now demonstrated by exhaustion rather than by keyword search.** The Coalition
  never posted publicly. This is itself a finding worth stating in the chronicle: by late 2017 the
  guild community's diplomacy had moved entirely to Discord, and the Coalition is the first major
  Wynncraft alliance with **no forum existence at all**.

### What the Coalition era *does* leave in writing
- **Guild alignments** (thread 227373, above): Fantasy [Fux] in; Sins of Seedia [SDU] a rebel against
  it; Fux later defected.
- **"the Coalition phase"** and **"when Coalition was taking its toll"** — the participant's own
  framing of it as a period of pressure that ended in the Federation's formation.
- **thread 265297-p02 #130** (2020): "Well there's only one Coalition and that died what like 2
  years ago" — dates its death to ~early 2018, consistent with the Federation's Feb 16 2018 founding.
- Round 1's founder-guild leader identifications (Killerfish/Gilded Sparrows, AurumKitsune/PHX,
  Pretzule/HoS, Drew1011/Foxes) stand; nothing new contradicts them, and nothing new confirms them.

## HOLE 5 — Libertas [Lir]. Partly closed: its *politics* are now known; its *wars* are still unwritten.

**What round 2 adds:**
- Libertas was **Hax's senior partner**, not an independent #2 power. Thread 133666 OP (Jun 26 2016)
  describes a **"rule of two"**: "This enemy, known only has 'Hack Forums'… Soon after, another
  guild, **Libertas**, joined the surge. **The two guilds crushed all opposition and still rule with
  an iron fist.**" This reframes its 60 territories in Sep 2016 — it held them *as part of the Hax
  bloc*, which is why no one ever fought it and won.
- Confirmed from the other side: Blacklisted's own OP (thread 131893) lists Libertas first among its
  allies; UXs described its pact with "Hax and Lir" (thread 133666 #13).
- **Libertas's reputation, ATTESTED:** thread **147267** ("Need Members War With Liberats",
  7Red_Dragon7, **Aug 28 2016**) — a small guild publicly recruiting to fight Libertas over
  **Nessak**. Reply #2, **Zelefant**: "**Libertas is extremely powerful, i wouldnt try them to start
  off.**" Reply #5, **Kiieran**: "See if you can somehow do better than **the other 50 guilds
  exactly the same as yours that tried**…" This is the only written record of anyone attacking
  Libertas, and it records a named contested territory (Nessak) and the community's expectation that
  the attack would fail.
- **Glitchedslayer**, thread 143392 #13, Aug 9 2016: "I do know that especially since **I always talk
  to yost**" — yosteric5 was a well-connected figure across guild lines.
- Membership churn attested: Libertas recruited heavily from ex-Hax players (thread 119596 pages 4,
  11, 21, 23, 25 all contain applicants naming Hax as a previous guild). The two guilds shared a
  talent pool as well as a border.

**Still missing:** any account of a war Libertas fought, any roster beyond the three officers, and
any end-date. All 26 pages of thread 119596 are now archived; **pages 2–26 are applications only** —
round 1's sampling conclusion holds across the full thread, so this is now settled rather than
suspected.
