# Dossier — Drew1011 "storytime", a recovered first-person account of 2015–2018

Research pass **2026-09-03**. Subject document: `data/sources/docs/drew1011-storytime.md`
(archive id **`drew1011-storytime`**, kind `testimony`, 1180 lines). Cross-checked against the
229-source archive this pass; new archive material found and cited below.

## What this source is

A serialised chat-history of Wynncraft guild politics posted by **Drew1011** into a guild
Discord channel between **22 Feb 2018** and **1 Feb 2021**, covering **early Jan 2015 to late
Jun 2018**. Drew1011 founded the Federation, led Valkyrie from Oct 2016, owned Holders of LE,
was King and then Viceroy of the Kingdom of Foxes, and sat in the Ordo Malleus leader chat.
**He is a participant in nearly everything he narrates**, and he narrates several disputes to
which he was a party.

**Provenance tier: RETROSPECTIVE (written), PARTISAN, SINGLE-SOURCE by default.** Not
contemporaneous, but *written*, *dated at paragraph level* (Discord timestamps survive in-line),
and by a named participant who states his own method. It sits above `RECOLLECTION` (a forum post
years later) and below `ATTESTED` (a contemporaneous thread). Where it agrees with an archived
thread, cite the thread. Where it is alone, the prose must say it is alone.

**He states his own standard, twice. Both quotes should govern how we use him:**

> "This is not an entirely subjective story, told completely by my opinions. Nor is this a
> biographical tale, devoid of any opinions of the author. Rather, it is a mixture of both. I
> strive to tell a factually accurate story, with no lies or complete falsities. Along with this
> factually accurate story, I interweave my own perspective and opinions… In no part of this
> story will this be made more apparent than in the collapse of Ordo. Parts of it will contain
> my biased perspective. If you ask others who were here, they might give a completely different
> perspective. But by no means is that proof that the stories I tell are factually incorrect."
> — L393–397

> "I will not go in to the details of said drama, as I was not part of it and have not heard the
> story from anyone without massive bias. I do not want to give you guys a story based entirely
> on biased hearsay, so I won't." — L542–543

**He states his own sourcing.** For the Ordo chapters he worked from a written timeline plus the
recovered Ordo Malleus forum thread: "I asked Arenos if he could possibly request that the
deleted Ordo thread be recovered so that I could retrieve dates from it that could be used for my
history archive. It proved useful, as **many of the dates and minor events during the Ordo parts
of this story would have been educated guesses without it**" (L695). He also kept a "timeline"
document and omitted events not written on it (L261). **Consequence: the Nov 2015 – Mar 2016
dates are second-hand from a forum thread and are better than memory — and this pass confirmed
several of them to the day. The 2017–2018 dates are memory, and he says so: "it has been almost 3
years at this point, and my memory of everything other than significant events has almost
entirely vanished" (L1153).**

**Line numbers.** All `L…` references are `cat -n` line numbers in
