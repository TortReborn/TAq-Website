# Research Dossier: 2019–2020 Alliance Churn (SOL / Luna / Terra / CoCK / Horologium / Artemis) + 1.20 Gavel Reborn

Generated 2026-09-03. Beat: the alliance churn between the Federation's collapse (Nov 2018) and the
1.20 guild-war rework (20 Jan 2021). Continues `research-2018-2019.md`; that dossier's §7 dead-end list
is revisited item by item in §8 below.

Provenance labels: **[ATTESTED]** = contemporaneous forum post (thread#, author, date, quote).
**[EDIT-DATED]** = text in a thread's opening post, datable only to that post's *last edit* (see the
methodology note in §0). **[RECOLLECTION]** = later retelling / memoir. **[MAP-INFERRED]** = from
territory-exchange data, not testimony. **[DEAD END]** = searched, nothing exists.

## 0. Two methodology findings that affect every citation

### 0a. The archive extractor renders an OP's date as its LAST-EDIT date

Confirmed on three threads. `thread-250066` shows `post #1 — GoatyGoatington — Sep 14, 2019` but its
*replies* begin `post #2 — Frost_Shadow_22 — May 30, 2019`; `thread-254001` shows
`post #1 — MultiSmith — Mar 9, 2020` with replies from Aug 20, 2019; `thread-254541` shows
`post #1 — VeeeKoooN — Mar 3, 2020` with replies from Sep 5, 2019. Cross-checked against the forum's
own thread-listing dates (e.g. the guild-wars tag page dates thread 282635 to Jan 22, 2021 while its
post #1 renders as Jan 26, 2021).

**Consequence:** a guild thread's OP text is evidence for its *last-edit* date, not the thread's
creation date. This is actually useful — it gives recruitment-thread boilerplate a hard timestamp —
but every claim drawn from an OP must be labelled **[EDIT-DATED]**. This also retroactively qualifies
S11 (Paladins United) in the previous dossier, where the concern was already flagged.

**It resolves an open question from `research-2018-2019.md` §3.** The Raiju thread's
`☾ RIP Luna Alliance ... ☾` line was flagged there as *"posted during the war; the RIP line may be a
later edit."* It is a later edit: the OP's last-edit stamp is **3 Mar 2020**, six months after Luna's
collapse. It is not a September 2019 document and must not be cited as one.

### 0b. Guest forum search is still unavailable — but the Alliance thread-prefix index is a full enumeration

`https://forums.wynncraft.com/search/?q=...` returns HTTP 200 but a register-wall for guests, so the
previous dossier's finding stands. However, the Guilds subforum exposes a thread-prefix filter, and
prefix **82 = "Alliance"**. Sorted oldest-first this is a *complete* list of every Alliance-prefixed
thread ever posted — 40 of them. Archived as
`forums-wynncraft-com-forums-guilds-72-prefix-id-82-order-post-date-direction-asc`.

This converts several soft "couldn't find it" dead ends into hard negative results (§8).

## 2. SOL — the hole is narrowed, not closed

### The find [ATTESTED]

**thread 256130, post #6, Pally (Titans Valor; also posts as PaladinSorceress — established by the
quote header in post #9), Nov 14 2019**, verbatim:

> "Most wars post-federation were waged to either keep their guild at the top in territory/xpwise, or
> to free up space. **I can only think of DsQ v ANO and SOL v Valk wars made with hatred after that
> time.** I think wars waged over xp and emeralds are more realistic than wars waged over hatreds.
> Nonetheless, we appear to be devolving from that golden state post Federation when there were no
> huge alliances that dictated the map, and appear to be waging more wars on hatred than before."

This is the **fourth attestation of SOL and the first that assigns it a role**: SOL fought Valkyrie,
and Pally — a war participant on the Terra/ANO side — classes it as one of only two grudge wars of the
whole post-Federation period. It independently corroborates the DB's framing that **Luna was "formed
on 14 Feb 2019 to counter SOL"**: the counter-alliance's own bloc name (Valk) is the one Pally puts on
the other side of the SOL war. Previously that causal link rested on the community Timeline image alone.

**New unresolved entity: "DsQ".** A guild or bloc that fought Titans Valor in a grudge war in the same
window. Not in `guild-prefix-overrides.json`. Player-page / map-data candidate.

### What is still missing

No roster, no leader, no founding date, no tag expansion. Searched and found nothing: the Alliance
prefix index (§0b), the alliance and guild-alliance tag pages, the 2019 Wayback RSS captures, the whole
local archive (`SOL` matches only `thread-251543` plus false positives like "solo"/"solution"), and web
search. **The current member list stays map-inferred.**

One structural observation worth recording: **the era's alliances are named for celestial bodies** —
SOL (sun), Luna (moon), Terra (earth), Horologium (a constellation), Artemis (moon goddess). SOL, Luna
and Terra form a deliberate set. This makes it very likely SOL was named by, or in the same circle as,
the people who named Luna, and it argues against SOL being a pre-existing acronym.

## 3. The treaties — indirect but real progress on Rodoroc

### Treaty of Rodoroc (effective 2 Mar 2019, Kingdom Foxes + DiamondDeities)

No thread records the treaty. **But the same Pally post (thread 256130 #6, Nov 14 2019) explains the
war the treaty must have ended** [ATTESTED]:

> "take for instance the Fox v DDT war #1 and #2 (electric boogaloo) those wars were waged because:
> War #1 : DDT had claim over LL (I believe) which pushed out a ton of emeralds and xp back then.
> War #2 : 1.18 had come out, and Fox wanted to kick DDT out of Molten as it was making nearly 20
> billion on days (due to d-cave parties in Molten Reach)"

**Rodoroc is the dwarven city in the Molten Heights.** A Kingdom Foxes–DiamondDeities instrument named
for a Molten Heights location, effective March 2019, sits exactly on top of a documented Fox-vs-DDT war
over Molten Heights territory driven by Molten Reach dungeon-cave income. This is strong circumstantial
support for reading the Treaty of Rodoroc as **the settlement of the second Fox–DDT war, partitioning
or conceding the Molten Heights**.

State it as: the treaty's *subject matter* is now evidenced; its *terms and signatories* remain
unrecorded. Do not print the partition reading as fact — Pally never names the treaty.

## 8. 1.20 Gavel Reborn (20 Jan 2021) — the hole is fully closed

### What actually changed (`thread-277744` changelog; `wynncraft-wiki-gg-wiki-version-1-20`)

The rework replaced essentially the entire territorial economy:

- **"Territories no longer generate XP."** XP became a *purchasable per-territory bonus* instead.
- **"No more War servers."** Wars moved into the world; *"instead, you have to defeat the war tower."*
- **Guild towers became upgradeable** with defenses, resources and special abilities.
- **Headquarters territories + trade routes**: excess resources route one territory at a time toward
  the HQ; guilds tax other guilds using their territories; *"enemy guild can cut off your trade route."*
- **Attacking a territory costs guild emeralds** (initially personal emeralds — see below).
- Guild bank; 4-letter tags; guild creation raised to **20 LE**; territory map on `L`.
- A later revision in the same changelog reduced tower upgrade prices and replaced the attack cost with
  a scaling HQ-emerald system (free first territory, price rising with holdings).

**This is the mechanism that dismantled the 2019–2020 alliance order.** Territory XP was the entire
economic basis of the claims system — the "unwritten rule" Pally described in Dec 2019 (§2), where
*"these territories pump emeralds and exp into your guild"* and attacking a claim triggers retaliation.
Removing territory XP removed the thing claims were *for*, and removing FFA XP removed the growth path
that GoulartJJ and Carrie had both cited as how a small guild could rise under Artemis.

### Community reaction [all ATTESTED]

**xMini_ (Aequitas), `thread-282635`, thread created 22 Jan 2021 / OP last edited 26 Jan 2021** — the
first-wave critique, from inside the map-holding bloc:

> "If we were to try and attack Lava Lake, the timer to start the war would be more than 40 minutes…
> These long timers prevent wars from happening all across the map."

> "Now with the 1.20 wars, **there are no FFA's as terrs no longer give xp** (another dumb idea). So
> basically its at a point now where you aren't warring unless you're being attacked."

> "**mega alliances are what made warring in Wynn fun and exciting in the first place.** Now that it's
> gone, a lot of the action and excitement is gone."

> "Subs were awesome and very useful for guilds. They could turn the tide of the battle and some subs
> even became their own guild, thus diversifying the warring community."

Note his framing of the political situation on 22 Jan 2021: *"Look at Goose right now. We have the
large majority of the map"* — placing xMini_/Aequitas in Goose, two days after the update.

**_Eragon_, `thread-286375`, 20 Feb 2021** — a month on, the anti-monopoly case that the rework *failed*:

> "If a mega alliance is already in control of the map then, there is no real chance for smaller guilds
> to ever hold a territory longer than the cool down… yesterday we were warring in Rymek, and after we
> had taken 4 territories, the price went up from 4096 emeralds to 24k emeralds."

> "the only way a guild can reliably take out any guild tower medium defense or above is if that war
> team is equipped with mythics… **the guild update shifted from repetitive wars and an environment
> where it was hard to establish smaller guilds on the map, to an uncreative 'build meta' and an
> environment where it is nearly impossible to establish smaller guilds on the map.**"

He also quotes a low-level member on the new level floor: *"i was warring with lvl 47 plus levels… i
feel like frigging hawkeye from the avengers; a useless archer who can't help the rest of the team."*

**IceResistance (Titans Valor founder), `thread-284184` "War Costs + Balances", 31 Jan 2021** — and this
one has an outcome:

> "**UPDATE: This has been changed, and the entire system is completely free to use now.**"
> "The 1.20 update has made guilds into Wynncraft's new money sink… In order to participate in the new
> war system, your members are forced to dish out their own emeralds — as if the new 20 le guild cost
> wasn't bad enough."

So the warring community's pressure produced a documented patch: the personal-emerald war cost was
removed within roughly two weeks. Followed by `thread-286748` "Pity Cost" (24 Feb 2021).

**Convergent verdict across both camps:** the pro-alliance side (xMini_) and the anti-monopoly side
(_Eragon_) agreed the rework did *not* break map monopolies, and both blamed long HQ-distance timers,
tower difficulty and the death of territory XP. That is the community reaction in one line.
